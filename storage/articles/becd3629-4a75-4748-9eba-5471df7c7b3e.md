An enchanted fountain that draws water from an underground river. This fountain uses gravitational magic to force the water upstream out of the fountain, whilst purifying it at the same time.

The fountain is constantly monitored by the Mages who manage the @[Somarth, School of the Arcane](location:0c485ca9-8af8-4950-ba1a-e342d466fda5) not to far from the fountain iself.
