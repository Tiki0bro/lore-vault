[h1]The Radiant Host (The Divine Blades)[/h1]
The Radiant Host is the martial arm of The Conclave, comprised of elite warriors known as Pulsaris. They are the Vanguard of the Pulse, acting as the physical manifestation of divine justice and protection. They are exclusively devoted to Ethos or Khemos.

[h2]The Two Sacred Orders[/h2]
The Radiant Host is divided into two exclusive orders, each with a distinct combat philosophy.

[h3]The Sunsworn Knights:[/h3] Pulsaris dedicated to Ethos. Their training philosophy is "The Unbreakable Phalanx," emphasizing discipline, resilience, and methodical advance. They are clad in gleaming heavy armor, their weapons and bodies infused with the radiant energy of Ethos. They swear oaths of truth and justice, serving as righteous smiters of heretics and protectors of the innocent.

[h3]The Moonguard: [/h3]Pulsaris dedicated to Khemos. Their training philosophy is "The Silent Watch," focusing on agility, stealth, and strategic intervention. They wear lighter armor and specialize in subtle combat, dispelling curses, and protecting sacred sites. They operate covertly, relying on stealth and surprise to fulfill their sacred oaths.

[h2]Training and Purpose[/h2]
Pulsaris undergo a rigorous regimen of martial and spiritual training, learning to channel their deity's will directly into combat. Their oaths are unbreakable commitments to their gods, with their power directly tied to their unwavering faith. They are the frontline force that defends the kingdom from threats that defy the spiritual and legal authority of The Conclave, and their leadership is split between a Grand Marshal of Ethos and a Grand Marshal of Khemos.
