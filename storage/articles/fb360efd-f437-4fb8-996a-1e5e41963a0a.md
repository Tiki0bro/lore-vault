The Godpulse is the omnipresent force that is the source of all @[Magic](template:2fb7fd89-0e02-4e47-bef5-bf09bcc4e52b) in the world of @[Dalari](template:1c118e12-4edb-4600-9e38-402a9a69b304) . It is described as the original heartbeat of creation that occurred when the First Light shattered the void .[br]
[br]
To the @[Gods](template:591a5a16-0dc5-4468-aee7-5fdc45ff2055) of @[Dalari](template:1c118e12-4edb-4600-9e38-402a9a69b304), the Godpulse is a living equation of perfect symmetry and motion . They perceive it not as a beat or a song, but as a complex geometric order . They believe mortals misinterpret this fundamental force because their senses are imperfect .

The @[Gods](template:591a5a16-0dc5-4468-aee7-5fdc45ff2055)' unique understanding comes from their journey. Having ascended from mortality by attaining a "God Spark," they were once flawed beings who now perceive the cosmic order in its purest form . @[Magic](template:2fb7fd89-0e02-4e47-bef5-bf09bcc4e52b), to a god, is the language of form, and to manipulate it is to rewrite the very grammar of existence itself . For example, the God of the Sun and Light, @[Ethos](template:01ebc462-0f1e-46bb-87b3-f86e08b87be0), would see the Godpulse as a constant, radiant source of energy that can be channeled with perfect precision . This geometric understanding reflects his domain and is a natural progression from a mortal life that may have valued perfect lines and angles. In contrast, the @[First Shadow](template:875055c4-75f7-41a4-a2a9-8fc4ddef0975), a being with a fragmented soul, perceives the Godpulse as a chaotic and broken echo of its true form, which stands in @[Stark](template:9d77652e-35d7-47b0-b65b-d1bd4f3ba72e) opposition to the @[Gods](template:591a5a16-0dc5-4468-aee7-5fdc45ff2055)' view .

[h2]Perceptions of the Godpulse[/h2]
Each race in @[Dalari](template:1c118e12-4edb-4600-9e38-402a9a69b304) perceives and interprets the Godpulse differently, through their own senses, myths, and metaphysics . No single perception is false, as each is just a fragment of a greater whole .

[h2]@[Gods](template:591a5a16-0dc5-4468-aee7-5fdc45ff2055): [/h2]The @[Gods](template:591a5a16-0dc5-4468-aee7-5fdc45ff2055) of @[Dalari](template:1c118e12-4edb-4600-9e38-402a9a69b304), who ascended from mortality , perceive the Godpulse not as a sound or rhythm but as a geometric equation of perfect symmetry and motion . They believe that mortals mishear it because their senses are imperfect .

[h2]Humans: [/h2]Humans perceive the Godpulse as a living rhythm, a constant thrum within the world and themselves . They call it @@[The Pulse Divine](template:11b07ea1-f4df-4a42-9cd5-fd0f59027a30)(article), and theologians believe it is the "rhythm by which the @[Gods](template:591a5a16-0dc5-4468-aee7-5fdc45ff2055) remember us" .

[h2]Dwarves: [/h2]To the dwarves, @[Magic](template:2fb7fd89-0e02-4e47-bef5-bf09bcc4e52b) is pressure and resonance . They feel the Godpulse as vibrations through metal and bedrock, calling it @[The Deepbeat](article:d3db39c4-b2d0-43e2-b147-9d514c1e2215) or Stone's Memory .

[h2]Elves:[/h2] Elves hear the Godpulse as a melody, which they call @[Lifesong](article:add773c7-4d67-4607-a335-bb94b32a5d19), the eternal harmony between life, death, and rebirth .

[h2]Orcs & the Ashen League:[/h2] The Ashen peoples perceive the Godpulse as a fire that never cools, calling it @[Emberflow](article:681a786d-2f95-4c19-886a-f8c6cb8b2f8a). They believe the @[Gods](template:591a5a16-0dc5-4468-aee7-5fdc45ff2055) gifted them fire instead of peace .

[h2]Norgn:[/h2] @[The Norgn](template:a30a5bc4-265c-432b-8dbe-e170cdf27ff2) believe @[Magic](template:2fb7fd89-0e02-4e47-bef5-bf09bcc4e52b) lies in absence and perceive the Godpulse as @[The Stillness](article:aafdc9b6-94b6-46bf-bbe5-c6db133e30ee)—the silent tension between breaths .

[h2]Meranth:[/h2] To the Meranth, the Godpulse manifests as invisible currents beneath the ocean's surface, which they call @@[The Undertide](template:2a9b7e52-0451-443c-865f-23517e8bd53e)(Article) .

[h2]Bloodline Magi: [/h2]Those born with innate power perceive the Godpulse not as a universal rhythm but as an inheritance encoded in their flesh . They call it @@[The Inheritance](template:8c21bdd0-c091-4267-9958-053d833999c7)(article) and believe they bear a god's fingerprint[br]
