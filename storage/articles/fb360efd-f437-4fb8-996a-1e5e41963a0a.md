The Godpulse is the omnipresent force that is the source of all magic in the world of Dalari . It is described as the original heartbeat of creation that occurred when the First Light shattered the void .[br]
[br]
To the gods of Dalari, the Godpulse is a living equation of perfect symmetry and motion . They perceive it not as a beat or a song, but as a complex geometric order . They believe mortals misinterpret this fundamental force because their senses are imperfect .

The gods' unique understanding comes from their journey. Having ascended from mortality by attaining a "God Spark," they were once flawed beings who now perceive the cosmic order in its purest form . Magic, to a god, is the language of form, and to manipulate it is to rewrite the very grammar of existence itself . For example, the God of the Sun and Light, Ethos, would see the Godpulse as a constant, radiant source of energy that can be channeled with perfect precision . This geometric understanding reflects his domain and is a natural progression from a mortal life that may have valued perfect lines and angles. In contrast, the First Shadow, a being with a fragmented soul, perceives the Godpulse as a chaotic and broken echo of its true form, which stands in stark opposition to the gods' view .

[h2]Perceptions of the Godpulse[/h2]
Each race in Dalari perceives and interprets the Godpulse differently, through their own senses, myths, and metaphysics . No single perception is false, as each is just a fragment of a greater whole .

[h2]Gods: [/h2]The gods of Dalari, who ascended from mortality , perceive the Godpulse not as a sound or rhythm but as a geometric equation of perfect symmetry and motion . They believe that mortals mishear it because their senses are imperfect .

[h2]Humans: [/h2]Humans perceive the Godpulse as a living rhythm, a constant thrum within the world and themselves . They call it @The Pulse Divine(article), and theologians believe it is the "rhythm by which the gods remember us" .

[h2]Dwarves: [/h2]To the dwarves, magic is pressure and resonance . They feel the Godpulse as vibrations through metal and bedrock, calling it @[The Deepbeat](article:d3db39c4-b2d0-43e2-b147-9d514c1e2215) or Stone's Memory .

[h2]Elves:[/h2] Elves hear the Godpulse as a melody, which they call @[Lifesong](article:add773c7-4d67-4607-a335-bb94b32a5d19), the eternal harmony between life, death, and rebirth .

[h2]Orcs & the Ashen League:[/h2] The Ashen peoples perceive the Godpulse as a fire that never cools, calling it @[Emberflow](article:681a786d-2f95-4c19-886a-f8c6cb8b2f8a). They believe the gods gifted them fire instead of peace .

[h2]Norgn:[/h2] The Norgn believe magic lies in absence and perceive the Godpulse as @[The Stillness](article:aafdc9b6-94b6-46bf-bbe5-c6db133e30ee)—the silent tension between breaths .

[h2]Meranth:[/h2] To the Meranth, the Godpulse manifests as invisible currents beneath the ocean's surface, which they call @The Undertide(Article) .

[h2]Bloodline Magi: [/h2]Those born with innate power perceive the Godpulse not as a universal rhythm but as an inheritance encoded in their flesh . They call it @The Inheritance(article) and believe they bear a god's fingerprint[br]
[br]
[h2]Dragons:[/h2] Beings that rival the Gods and the chaos lords view the Godpulse as @The Primal Breath(article)
