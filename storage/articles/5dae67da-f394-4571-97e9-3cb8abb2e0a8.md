Silverhawks are an elite and secretive army corp for the kingdom of Artilian. Silverhawks are selected and recruited from some of the best warriors in the kingdom. Silverhawks undertake dangerous missions at the request of the King and have often times throughout history have become the unsung heroes of the Kingdom. Although there are many who know of the silver hawks, very few have ever seen them, if you do see them... run the other way.[br]
Although silverhawks fight for glory and the welfare of the kingdom, they are still in fact just vessels to carry out the Kings personal orders whether they be honorable or whether are selfish.
