The Somarth, School of the Arcane is a branch of the great @Imperium(organization) organization. This is a local hub for Wizards, Sorcerers and Mages in Redcliff. This is a great place for those who are wanting to learn and study the Arcane. The school has a steep pricing but the payoff is worth it for those who don't want to travel to the capital city of @[Artilian](landmark:eab4652d-235b-4aa5-b1ae-e592ee8df26f). There are some great books that can be read only at the School and will require great raport with the teachers. 

They also have beginner Enchanters who are always trying to sell their wares. But you better be willing to spend some coin.
