[h1]The Emberflow: Internal Fire, External Storm[/h1]
The Emberflow is the Ashen League's (Orcs, Hobgoblins, Ogres) interpretation of the Godpulse: a burning current that fuels strength, rage, and vitality. Their magic is a dynamic, two-way process where intense emotion acts as a powerful pump, allowing them to draw in wild ambient energy or discharge their stored internal energy.

[h2]Internal Discharge (The Fury Buff)[/h2]
This is the power of self-enhancement, used primarily by warriors. It relies on the raw, accumulated chaotic elemental energy already stored within their blood and bone.

[h2]Mechanism: [/h2]The Orc focuses a sudden, intense burst of emotion (rage, passion). This emotion instantly metabolizes the stored elemental energy, forcibly injecting it into their muscle and nervous system. This is a purely internal effect—the elemental energy is discharged into the body, not out of it.

[h2]Practical Examples:[/h2]
[h3]Stone Skin: [/h3]Discharging accumulated Earth Particles into the dermal layers, causing the skin to momentarily harden like bedrock.
[h3]Lightning Strikes: [/h3]Discharging stored Electricity Particles into the nervous system, giving the warrior blinding speed for a brief series of strikes.
[h3]Vitality Surge: [/h3]Discharging raw Fire Particles into the blood to instantly cauterize a wound or shrug off crippling pain.

[h2]External Conduction (The Storm Caller)[/h2]
This is the power of elemental manipulation, used primarily by shamans. It relies on using the intense internal emotional fire as a conduit or magnet to manipulate chaotic external energy (similar to Druidic magic).

[h2]Mechanism:[/h2] The shaman uses intense, loud War-Chants to build their emotional energy. This fury acts like an unstable antenna, violently connecting with the wildest, most turbulent elemental particles in the surrounding environment.

[h2]Practical Examples:[/h2]
[h3]Creating a Lightning Bolt:[/h3] The shaman projects their rage skyward. This fury acts as a disruptive force, violently forcing unstable atmospheric Electricity Particles to discharge along the path of their will, creating a chaotic, jagged bolt of lightning.
[h3]Wildfire Surge: [/h3]The shaman focuses their fury on an already existing flame. Instead of harmonizing (like an Elf), they incite the flame's chaotic nature, causing it to explode into a torrent of unpredictable, wild fire streams.

[h2]The Role of Emotion[/h2]
In the Emberflow, emotion serves as the throttle. It's the only force strong enough to either violently tear the accumulated energy from the body (Internal Discharge) or forcibly rip wild ambient energy from the environment (External Conduction). Without the inner fire, the Emberflow is inert.

This dual nature makes the Ashen League formidable: their warriors are self-buffing engines of destruction, and their shamans are capable of calling down chaos.
