[h1]Divine Magic: The Consecrated Power[/h1]
Divine Magic is the type of magic that draws power from the Godpulse through the deities and their consecrated domains. Practitioners, known as @Theurges(article) or @Pulsaris(article), wield this power as a channeled gift, not a personal talent, making their magical strength utterly dependent on their spiritual connection and the vitality of their god.

[h2]The Principles of Divine Channeling[/h2]
Divine power operates through a unique, two-way feedback loop that separates it from all other magical philosophies:

[h2]1. The Godpulse Filter (The Divine Perception)[/h2]
The raw source of power remains the Godpulse, which the gods perceive as a perfect geometric equation of symmetry and motion.

[h3]Filtering: [/h3]When a deity channels magic to a believer, they act as a filter. They take the raw, infinite energy of the Godpulse and shape it according to their portfolio (domain). For instance, Ethos (God of Light) filters the energy into structured light and healing, while Khemos (God of Night) filters it into subtle shadow and restorative cycles.

[h3]Geometric Command: [/h3]The divine mind bypasses the need for human methods (like Elven melodies or Words of Power). A god's command is a direct, effortless manipulation of the Godpulse's fundamental geometry.

[h2]2. The Faith-Power Exchange[/h2]
The sheer power a god can channel is proportional to the size and intensity of their faith base.

[h3]theurge's Dependence:[/h3] A theurge's strength is directly tied to the collective belief in their deity. This creates a reliance on the god's status. If a god loses followers (loses influence), the magic granted to their Theurges immediately diminishes, regardless of the theurge's personal devotion.

[h3]The Power Ladder:[/h3] This is why Greater Gods (like Ethos) can grant more potent, large-scale magic than a Lesser God (like Zylos). The larger the faith, the stronger the pipeline from the Godpulse.

[h3]Practical Use: Ritual and Petition[/h3]
Divine magic is not a formula (like the Aetheric Frequency) or an instinct (like The Inheritance); it is an act of petition and devotion.

[h3]Establishing the Connection[/h3]
To cast divine magic, a theurge or priest must first establish a clear connection with their deity.

[h3]Ritual: [/h3]This usually involves prayer, meditation, or a specific religious ritual associated with the god’s domain. A theurge of Khemos, for example, might be required to cast their healing spells under the light of the moon or during the quiet hours of the night.

[h3]The Consecrated Focus:[/h3] Theurges invariably use a consecrated object (a holy symbol, amulet, or prayer beads) that acts as a secure antenna for the divine channel. This symbol is often a physical representation of the god's geometric ideal (e.g., a perfect sunburst for Ethos).
[h3]The Act of Casting[/h3]
When a spell is cast, the theurge is essentially asking their god to briefly manipulate the Godpulse on the mortal plane on their behalf.

[h3]Petition: [/h3]The theurge speaks a prayer or verse related to the god’s portfolio. This sound isn't the Word of Power that commands energy; it's the petition that asks the god to channel the filtered energy.

[h3]Manifestation: [/h3]The magic manifests as a highly specialized effect associated with the domain. A theurge of Ethos might radiate blinding light and protective wards, while a theurge of Malcor (God of Disease) would use their magic to induce decay and sickness .
