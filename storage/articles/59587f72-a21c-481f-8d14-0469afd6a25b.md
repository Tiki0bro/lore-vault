Home to the @[Raven Queen](person:f19a6e2f-ef62-4a26-a177-9ae77e1d48a1) her children the @[Aya-la](template:6375f1b0-7638-464a-85ae-413f72d69a45).

After stealing a portion of the First Shadows power, the @[Raven Queen](person:f19a6e2f-ef62-4a26-a177-9ae77e1d48a1) created the @[Aya-la](template:6375f1b0-7638-464a-85ae-413f72d69a45)-mua (Her first born offspring) these children possessed a high concentration of shadow @[Magic](template:2fb7fd89-0e02-4e47-bef5-bf09bcc4e52b) and were used by the @[Raven Queen](template:f19a6e2f-ef62-4a26-a177-9ae77e1d48a1) to conquer the surrounding land. They killed thousands of shadow creatures and expanded their control of the surrounding territory, through blooshed and war they carved out a territory which have since now become @[The Shaded Isles](template:80f9b011-6cc9-4a96-91b5-abe3ac83cd47), The @[Raven Queen](person:f19a6e2f-ef62-4a26-a177-9ae77e1d48a1)'s quick expansion caught the attention of the nearby @Lords of Shadow(Article), 3 of Which decided to pay the @[Raven Queen](person:f19a6e2f-ef62-4a26-a177-9ae77e1d48a1) a visit, they threatened her with war if she refused to stop expanding her territory and forces. The @[Raven Queen](person:f19a6e2f-ef62-4a26-a177-9ae77e1d48a1) conceded and decided to halt her expansion within the @[Shadow Realm](template:fc24f94d-20e2-4ed5-9b1c-1ec45db12892) and has ever since been passively waiting and building her forces until the right time to strike again.
