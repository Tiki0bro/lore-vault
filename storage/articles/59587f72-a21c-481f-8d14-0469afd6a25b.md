Home to the @[Raven Queen](person:f19a6e2f-ef62-4a26-a177-9ae77e1d48a1) her children the Aya-la.

After stealing a portion of the First Shadows power, the @[Raven Queen](person:f19a6e2f-ef62-4a26-a177-9ae77e1d48a1) created the Aya-la-mua (Her first born offspring) these children possessed a high concentration of shadow magic and were used by the Raven Queen to conquer the surrounding land. They killed thousands of shadow creatures and expanded their control of the surrounding territory, through blooshed and war they carved out a territory which have since now become the Shaded Isles, The @[Raven Queen](person:f19a6e2f-ef62-4a26-a177-9ae77e1d48a1)'s quick expansion caught the attention of the nearby @Lords of Shadow(Article), 3 of Which decided to pay the @[Raven Queen](person:f19a6e2f-ef62-4a26-a177-9ae77e1d48a1) a visit, they threatened her with war if she refused to stop expanding her territory and forces. The @[Raven Queen](person:f19a6e2f-ef62-4a26-a177-9ae77e1d48a1) conceded and decided to halt her expansion within the Shadow Realm and has ever since been passively waiting and building her forces until the right time to strike again.
