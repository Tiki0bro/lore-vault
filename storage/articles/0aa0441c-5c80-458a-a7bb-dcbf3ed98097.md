The Silverhawk Emblem is an emblem given to those who serve under the @[Silverhawks](organization:5dae67da-f394-4571-97e9-3cb8abb2e0a8) corp. Those who bear this emblem are under direct orders from the King himself, this emblem provides rights and authorities to act in the kings name. 

The Emblem is a small palm sized talisman made of gold and silver, with the head of hawk engraved into the talisman.
