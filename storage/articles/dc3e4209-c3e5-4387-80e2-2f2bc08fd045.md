Archmagi are powerful wielders of elemental magic. These powerful mages can rearrange the very fabric of reality on a small scale. 

Archmage is a Kingdom title to those who have become a grandmaster in a single school of Magic. The title of Archmage is only given to one who has joined the Imperium though. Mages and Wizards that have attained grandmaster status outside of the Imperium are called @Renegade Archmagi(Article) these renegades are often feared because they have no alliance with the Imperium and are thus considered dangerous. These Archmagi are often hunted down by the Imperium's own Archmagi if a danger to the kingdom or are forced to join the imperium, otherwise in some cases they are captured and imprisoned.
