Situated South East of @[Fort Redcliff](location:5a4dd25a-0ef1-4fc1-85b8-8de8b2d884e3) The Twinpine Garrison guards the @[Belrund Bridge](landmark:c7fa385e-3ca6-4160-8ee5-9135444e9ca5) bridge leading towards Thitslewood and the Belrund Mines. They patrol the surrounding region esspecially the southern forests as they are filled with danger.
