[h2]Unique Abilities[/h2]

[b]Lithic Charge (The Horn-Hammer)[/b]
The prowler's namesake crest is a dense, horn-like protrusion made of calcified mineral-bone. By vibrating this crest at a specific harmonic, the Prowler can "harden" its own cellular structure. A full-speed charge from a Prowler has the impact force of a falling boulder, allowing it to smash through fortified gates or rhino-hide armor.

[b]Seismic Shadow-Step[/b]
Despite their weight, their paws are cushioned by soft, sound-dampening pads. They use "Seismic Resonance" to match their footfalls to the natural vibrations of the cliff-face, making them functionally silent even to the most sensitive elven ears.

[b]Stone-Sight (Deepbeat Radar)[/b]
By pressing their crest against a solid surface, they can send a pulse of energy through the rock. The returning "echo" provides a perfect 3D mental map of everything within the cliff, including hidden chambers, underground rivers, or hiding enemies.

[h2]Habitat & Behavior[/h2]
They primarily dwell in the narrow, wind-swept caves of the mid-tier cliffs. They are intensely territorial but form deep, life-long bonds with Beast-Callers who can match their "Deepbeat" resonance.
[h2]Harmonization: The Anthem of the Mountain’s Root[/h2]
A unique challenge for the Elves of Aether's Perch is bridging the gap between their native [b]@[Lifesong](template:add773c7-4d67-4607-a335-bb94b32a5d19)[/b] (high-frequency melody) and the Prowler's [b]Deepbeat[/b] (low-frequency tectonic resonance).

[b]Sub-Harmonic Threading[/b]
Beast-Callers utilize a technique called [i]Sub-Harmonic Threading[/i]. They learn to sing the [b]"Anthem of the Mountain’s Root,"[/b] a guttural variant of the @[Lifesong](template:add773c7-4d67-4607-a335-bb94b32a5d19) that resonates deep in the chest. This pitch is low enough to catch the upper vibrations of @[The Deepbeat](template:d3db39c4-b2d0-43e2-b147-9d514c1e2215).

[b]The Resonant Interval[/b]
The Prowler's crest acts as a biological tuning fork, catching the Elf's Anthem of the Mountain’s Root and amplifying it. This creates a shared "Resonant Interval" where the two spirits lock together. The Elf provides the tactical [i]Melody[/i] (intent and direction), while the Prowler provides the physical [i]Bass[/i] (power and momentum). In this state, the rider and mount move as a single, unstoppable force of nature.
