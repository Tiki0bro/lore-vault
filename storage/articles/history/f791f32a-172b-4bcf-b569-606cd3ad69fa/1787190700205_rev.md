Description: An imposing man with a heavy stainless steel prosthetic right hand, he is wearing a long, white cloak. His face is scarred and worn.

@[Personality](template:20338017-68ac-4ea4-9264-f3c2d3656c26): He is extraordinarily eccentric. He is always fidgeting with his prosthetic hand and constantly makes clicking noises with his fingers. 

History: When he was but 4 years old his parents immigrated from west march east to Recliff. As a young child he wandered into the woods despite his mother's warnings against it. Afterwards he claimed he had encountered dire rats who tried and succesfully ate his hand. He had a knack for the arcane arts and eventually travelled to @[Artilian](template:eab4652d-235b-4aa5-b1ae-e592ee8df26f) and joined the @[Imperium](template:f0b5530f-347c-4467-81fb-3688af0bd6fc), after joining the @[Imperium](template:f0b5530f-347c-4467-81fb-3688af0bd6fc) he was able to research and create for himself a prosthetic hand using @[Magic](template:2fb7fd89-0e02-4e47-bef5-bf09bcc4e52b) and enchanting. For many years he taught at the @[Imperium](template:f0b5530f-347c-4467-81fb-3688af0bd6fc) in @[Artilian](template:eab4652d-235b-4aa5-b1ae-e592ee8df26f) until he was put up for a promotion and became the current @[Archmage](template:dc3e4209-c3e5-4387-80e2-2f2bc08fd045) for the @[Somarth, School of the Arcane](location:0c485ca9-8af8-4950-ba1a-e342d466fda5), Without a family of his own to hold him back, he immediately made the journey and was able to relocate to @[Fort Redcliff](location:5a4dd25a-0ef1-4fc1-85b8-8de8b2d884e3).
