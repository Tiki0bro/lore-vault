[h1]@[The Undertide](template:c770ba26-dbac-4995-953b-9121e31b8b78): Meranth @[Magic](template:2fb7fd89-0e02-4e47-bef5-bf09bcc4e52b) of Currents and Memory[/h1]
In the endless deeps, the Meranth (Sea Peoples) hear no beat, song, or resonance. To them, the @[Godpulse](template:fb360efd-f437-4fb8-996a-1e5e41963a0a) manifests as @[The Undertide](template:c770ba26-dbac-4995-953b-9121e31b8b78)—invisible currents and flows beneath the ocean's skin. They believe @[The Undertide](template:c770ba26-dbac-4995-953b-9121e31b8b78) is the very memory of the sea, constantly moving unseen but shaping all that drifts above.

[h2]Philosophical Foundation: Flow and Continuity[/h2]
The Meranth philosophy is rooted in the belief that all existence is a continuous, fluid motion. @[Magic](template:2fb7fd89-0e02-4e47-bef5-bf09bcc4e52b) is not something commanded; it is a current that must be ridden.

[h2]The Path of Least Resistance:[/h2] Meranth casters, often called "Tide-Seers," achieve their effects by understanding the path of least resistance. Instead of creating a magical force, they find the nearest existing magical flow within @[The Undertide](template:c770ba26-dbac-4995-953b-9121e31b8b78) and gently pull it or push it into the desired effect.

[h2]@[Magic](template:2fb7fd89-0e02-4e47-bef5-bf09bcc4e52b) and Memory: [/h2]They believe the deepest, slowest currents of @[The Undertide](template:c770ba26-dbac-4995-953b-9121e31b8b78) hold the memory of all life that has passed through the water. Accessing these currents allows for powerful divination and historical sight, revealing truths long hidden beneath the waves.

[h2]Practical Application: Riding the Flow[/h2]
Meranth spellcasting is a meditative and highly fluid act that relies on subtle motions and perfect timing with the sea's natural cycles.

[h2]Silent Casting (Riding @[The Undertide](template:c770ba26-dbac-4995-953b-9121e31b8b78)): [/h2]Tide-Seers seldom use verbal incantations. Instead, they use small, graceful, yet firm movements of their hands, fins, or tails to "pull" or "push" the magical currents. To cast a potent effect, the caster finds a powerful flow in @[The Undertide](template:c770ba26-dbac-4995-953b-9121e31b8b78) and allows it to "pull their thoughts into action," manifesting the spell with immense, effortless force.

[h2]The Tidal Anchor:[/h2] Meranth rituals are often tied to the cycles of the moon and the tides. They perform deep-sea meditations to allow @[The Undertide](template:c770ba26-dbac-4995-953b-9121e31b8b78) to cleanse and attune their consciousness. The strength of their @[Magic](template:2fb7fd89-0e02-4e47-bef5-bf09bcc4e52b) waxes and wanes with the ocean's cycles, making them most powerful during the deepest, strongest tidal pulls.

[h2]Defensive @[Magic](template:2fb7fd89-0e02-4e47-bef5-bf09bcc4e52b): [/h2]Meranth defense is primarily based on misdirection and flow. They can create localized turbulence to disrupt an enemy's movement or use @[The Undertide](template:c770ba26-dbac-4995-953b-9121e31b8b78) to push hostile spells off course, making them masters of environmental control in their domain.

[h2]Contrasting @[The Undertide](template:c770ba26-dbac-4995-953b-9121e31b8b78) Vs. @[The Pulse Divine](template:11b07ea1-f4df-4a42-9cd5-fd0f59027a30) (Humans): [/h2]The Meranth view the landfolk as foolish for chasing "storms above" while they "swim in the storm that never ends". They see the human emphasis on measurable frequency (Aetheric Frequency) as rigid and limited, missing the constant, fluid change that defines reality.

[h2]Vs. @[The Deepbeat](template:d3db39c4-b2d0-43e2-b147-9d514c1e2215) (Dwarves):[/h2] The concept of locking @[Magic](template:2fb7fd89-0e02-4e47-bef5-bf09bcc4e52b) into fixed material (like @[Runes](template:22a74c4e-99e3-4103-9d1e-b2d9f225c901) on stone) is antithetical to the Meranth. To them, permanence is impossible; all things must flow and change.

[h2]Casting a Barrier:[/h2] A human mage creates a structured wall of force. A Meranth Tide-Seer creates a momentary, localized whirlpool to catch and divert an incoming attack, demonstrating the superiority of flow over rigidity.
