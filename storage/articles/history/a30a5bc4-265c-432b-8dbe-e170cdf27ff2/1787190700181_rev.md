[h1]The Norgn[/h1]
The [b]Norgn[/b] are a tall, pale-skinned human ethnicity inhabiting the frozen northern reaches of @[Dalari](template:1c118e12-4edb-4600-9e38-402a9a69b304). Forged by the relentless tundra and the biting winds of the northern crags, they have developed a unique relationship with the @[url=article:fb360efd-f437-4fb8-996a-1e5e41963a0a]@[Godpulse](template:fb360efd-f437-4fb8-996a-1e5e41963a0a)[/url], viewing it not as a flow of energy, but as a frozen potential waiting to be revealed.

[h2]I. Cultural Philosophy: @[The Stillness](template:aafdc9b6-94b6-46bf-bbe5-c6db133e30ee)[/h2]
Unlike other cultures that view @[Magic](template:2fb7fd89-0e02-4e47-bef5-bf09bcc4e52b) as a rushing current or a burning flame, the Norgn interpret the @[url=article:fb360efd-f437-4fb8-996a-1e5e41963a0a]@[Godpulse](template:fb360efd-f437-4fb8-996a-1e5e41963a0a)[/url] as [b]“@[The Stillness](template:aafdc9b6-94b6-46bf-bbe5-c6db133e30ee)”[/b]—a state of perfect, latent potential. Their @[Magic](template:2fb7fd89-0e02-4e47-bef5-bf09bcc4e52b), practiced by specialized [b]Ice-Seers[/b], involves the revelation of existing possibilities rather than the aggressive creation of new energy. To a Norgn, a spell is not cast; it is [i]uncovered[/i].

[h2]III. Strengths & Vulnerabilities[/h2]
[b]Anchor of Kinship:[/b] The @[Magic](template:2fb7fd89-0e02-4e47-bef5-bf09bcc4e52b) of the Norgn is deeply tied to their social fabric. Familial trust and communal bonds act as a stabilizing force for their clarity, allowing multiple Seers to link their minds in a shared vision of @[The Stillness](template:aafdc9b6-94b6-46bf-bbe5-c6db133e30ee).

[b]Vulnerability to Noise:[/b] Because their @[Magic](template:2fb7fd89-0e02-4e47-bef5-bf09bcc4e52b) relies on absolute mental and spiritual quiet, they are uniquely vulnerable to [i]Dissonance[/i]. Emotional shock, sudden chaos, or loud magical "static" (such as the percussive strikes of @[url=article:d3db39c4-b2d0-43e2-b147-9d514c1e2215]@[The Deepbeat](template:d3db39c4-b2d0-43e2-b147-9d514c1e2215)[/url]) can shatter their clarity, leaving an Ice-Seer temporarily powerless or mentally scarred.
