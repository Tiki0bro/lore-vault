[h1]Bloodline @[Magic](template:2fb7fd89-0e02-4e47-bef5-bf09bcc4e52b): The Inheritance[/h1]
Bloodline @[Magic](template:2fb7fd89-0e02-4e47-bef5-bf09bcc4e52b), known to its practitioners as The Inheritance, is a primary branch of @[Magic](template:2fb7fd89-0e02-4e47-bef5-bf09bcc4e52b) in @[Dalari](template:1c118e12-4edb-4600-9e38-402a9a69b304). It is defined by the belief that magical power is not external energy to be learned or measured, but a divine residue encoded into the flesh and passed down through generations.

[h2]The Philosophy: Power is Remembered[/h2]
Practitioners of the Inheritance reject the doctrines of the @[Imperium](template:f0b5530f-347c-4467-81fb-3688af0bd6fc) and the @[Artilian](template:eab4652d-235b-4aa5-b1ae-e592ee8df26f) Kingdom. They hold that the @[Godpulse](template:fb360efd-f437-4fb8-996a-1e5e41963a0a) leaves a "fingerprint of the @[Gods](template:591a5a16-0dc5-4468-aee7-5fdc45ff2055)" in mortals, meaning power is not granted by devotion or formula, but is simply remembered—an awakening of ancestral memory.

[h2]Instinctual Casting:[/h2] Casting is purely instinctual, driven by raw emotion and innate will. There are no @[Words of Power](template:4db351c9-18ff-41db-a6d9-6887aa490947) or @[Spell Formations](template:762bed00-b3d7-4f2d-ba07-4d8275605066); the mage wills the @[Magic](template:2fb7fd89-0e02-4e47-bef5-bf09bcc4e52b) to happen, and the ancestral echo guides the power.

[h2]The Cost of Focus:[/h2] Using Bloodline @[Magic](template:2fb7fd89-0e02-4e47-bef5-bf09bcc4e52b) is draining, as it is tied directly to the caster's life essence and vitality. Furthermore, for the purest lines (like the House of Val'Raen), this intense focus creates Magical Isolation, making them magically deaf to all other interpretations of the @[Godpulse](template:fb360efd-f437-4fb8-996a-1e5e41963a0a) (@[Lifesong](template:add773c7-4d67-4607-a335-bb94b32a5d19), Deepbeat, Aetheric Frequency).

[h2]Lineages of Inheritance[/h2]
The Inheritance is categorized based on the origin and stability of the power:

[h2]1. Noble/Ancient Bloodlines (e.g., House of Val'Raen)[/h2]
Origin: Traces back to an ancient, often singular, accidental infusion of a pure spark of the @[Godpulse](template:fb360efd-f437-4fb8-996a-1e5e41963a0a) (e.g., from a shattered divine relic).

[h3]Characteristics: [/h3]Grants immense longevity, stability, and control over their singular power. Their @[Magic](template:2fb7fd89-0e02-4e47-bef5-bf09bcc4e52b) is highly focused, such as the pure Bloodline Telekinesis known as The Crimson Art. This stability makes them the natural leaders of other, more volatile bloodlines.

[h2]2. Cursed/Warlock Bloodlines (The Cursed Inheritance)[/h2]
Origin: Derived from a chaotic infusion through interspecies breeding with magical monsters (e.g., demons, nagas) or traumatic magical experimentation.

[h3]Characteristics: [/h3]Power is potent but highly volatile and often aesthetically jarring, bearing the physical mark of its monstrous source. Their @[Magic](template:2fb7fd89-0e02-4e47-bef5-bf09bcc4e52b) is chaotic and unpredictable, manifesting as unstable combustion (Ka'zhar) or untraceable venoms (Serpentis). They require the stability of the Noble Bloodlines to manage their power.

[h2]Conflict with the Establishment[/h2]
The Inheritance is violently opposed by the ruling powers:

[h3]Heresy (@[Artilian](template:eab4652d-235b-4aa5-b1ae-e592ee8df26f) Church):[/h3] The claim that @[Magic](template:2fb7fd89-0e02-4e47-bef5-bf09bcc4e52b) is an inherited birthright borders on heresy, as the Church insists the @[Godpulse](template:fb360efd-f437-4fb8-996a-1e5e41963a0a) is divine property.

[h3]Anathema (The @[Imperium](template:f0b5530f-347c-4467-81fb-3688af0bd6fc)): [/h3]The @[Magic](template:2fb7fd89-0e02-4e47-bef5-bf09bcc4e52b) is volatile, impossible to measure with Aetheric Frequency, and cannot be regulated by the @[Imperium](template:f0b5530f-347c-4467-81fb-3688af0bd6fc)'s scientific systems, making all Bloodline Magi a target.
