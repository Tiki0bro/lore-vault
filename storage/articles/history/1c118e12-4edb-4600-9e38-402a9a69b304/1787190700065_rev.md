[h2]I. Primary Lore & Details[/h2]
@[Dalari](template:bc52207c-5628-4fff-95da-99fdf2e9a6aa) is the largest continent in this world and typically thought of by its residents as the entirety of the world of @[Dalari](template:bc52207c-5628-4fff-95da-99fdf2e9a6aa), however there exists continents towards the south, the continent of Murr which is a smaller and unknown to the majority of the races that dwell on @[Dalari](template:bc52207c-5628-4fff-95da-99fdf2e9a6aa). These two continents are separted by a vast and unforgiving ocean and with the current technology these seas are inpossible to navigate, not only the storms that rage, but also the monsters that live deep within these oceans. Therefore the two continents have remained isolated from each other each developing at a different rate than the other.

@[Dalari](template:bc52207c-5628-4fff-95da-99fdf2e9a6aa) is a world of @[Magic](template:2fb7fd89-0e02-4e47-bef5-bf09bcc4e52b), Swords and Socerery and most importantly it is a world of @[Gods](template:591a5a16-0dc5-4468-aee7-5fdc45ff2055). The @[Gods](template:591a5a16-0dc5-4468-aee7-5fdc45ff2055) of @[Dalari](template:bc52207c-5628-4fff-95da-99fdf2e9a6aa) live on @[Dalari](template:bc52207c-5628-4fff-95da-99fdf2e9a6aa) but are secluded in their pocket dimensions or there own divine demi-planes. Some @[Gods](template:591a5a16-0dc5-4468-aee7-5fdc45ff2055) are friendly and guide the races of @[Dalari](template:bc52207c-5628-4fff-95da-99fdf2e9a6aa) towards a brighter future and some like to cause mischief and pain for its inhabitants and there are lots who just don't care and spend their days doing only what they feel like doing. You will find the worship of @[Gods](template:591a5a16-0dc5-4468-aee7-5fdc45ff2055) is the norm and part of everyday life, however there are just as many people who revile the @[Gods](template:591a5a16-0dc5-4468-aee7-5fdc45ff2055) in this world.

There are many races that live on the world of @[Dalari](template:bc52207c-5628-4fff-95da-99fdf2e9a6aa). We have humankind which have created an empire known as the "@[Artilian](template:eab4652d-235b-4aa5-b1ae-e592ee8df26f) Kingdom" which is a collection of city states under the rule of a single king. There are also a group of human city states on the west coast that are called the "Free Cities" which are a loosely allied group of cities that govern themselves and have no ties to the "@[Artilian](template:eab4652d-235b-4aa5-b1ae-e592ee8df26f) Kingdom" other than trading. The Fridged north is home to 3 independent Norgn Kingdoms each kingdom vying over the lands and riches that the other posseses, These 3 Kingdoms have been hostile towards eachother since before the "Chaos wars". Dwarves live within the mountains throughout @[Dalari](template:bc52207c-5628-4fff-95da-99fdf2e9a6aa) with the greatest of the cities being the Dwarven capital of @[Dwargen](template:8575247c-f649-4462-b757-e4904bf17784). The Dwarves outside of @[Dwargen](template:8575247c-f649-4462-b757-e4904bf17784) are all independent dwarven kingdoms in there own regards. In the "Blasted Plains" various races have allied themselves together to form an Alliance called the "Ashen League" a group comprised of orcs, hobgoblins, goblins and ogres, unfriendly towards outsiders. The "Ashen League" often have skirmishes with humans who tress pass on their land.
