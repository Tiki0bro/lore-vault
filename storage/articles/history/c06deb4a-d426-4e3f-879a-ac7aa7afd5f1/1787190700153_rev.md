Roland is the current Dean for the @[Somarth, School of the Arcane](location:0c485ca9-8af8-4950-ba1a-e342d466fda5) He is a well respected teacher and also an @[Archmage](article:dc3e4209-c3e5-4387-80e2-2f2bc08fd045). He is a grandmaster of Transmutation @[Magic](article:2fb7fd89-0e02-4e47-bef5-bf09bcc4e52b) and also a master of Evocation @[Magic](template:2fb7fd89-0e02-4e47-bef5-bf09bcc4e52b). As the current Dean he is responsible for overseeing the teachers and the students at the @[Somarth, School of the Arcane](location:0c485ca9-8af8-4950-ba1a-e342d466fda5). He is known to be a bit of a shut in and will often go days and weeks without leaving his laboratory as he conducts his various experiments. He is very wealthy due to his ability to create rare and magical Metals and Gems.

He has a prosthetic metallic hand which he often tries to hide under his cloak. This metallic arm is a magical item bound to roland and serves quite literally as his right hand man. 

Every month he will host a private lecture where he selects 6 students from a pool of students who have been recommended by the various teachers at the School. He then provides them with insights into the world of transmutation which is beneficial for those who seek this kind of wisdom.
