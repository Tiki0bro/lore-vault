[h1]Oakhaven[/h1]
Oakhaven is a strategic settlement situated within the @[Artilian](template:eab4652d-235b-4aa5-b1ae-e592ee8df26f) Delta. It serves as a vital transition point between the rugged highlands of @[Dwargen](template:8575247c-f649-4462-b757-e4904bf17784) and the marshy expanse of Redcliff. Known for its vertical architecture and its life-saving medicinal exports, the town is a masterpiece of adaptation to a fluctuating landscape.

[h2]Geography & Layout[/h2]
The town is defined by its striking [b]Verticality[/b]. Positioned on a series of limestone outcroppings where mountain runoff meets the lowlands, Oakhaven often faces seasonal flooding. To combat this, the citizens utilize the [b]High-Walks[/b]—a network of elevated boardwalks and suspension bridges that weave through the canopy.

The [b]Great Father Oak[/b] serves as the central anchor for the town's structural integrity. This ancient, magically-resilient tree provides the primary supports for the upper residential tiers and the administrative hubs.

[h2]Industry & Unique Features[/h2]
The economy of Oakhaven revolves around the [b]Iron-Oak[/b] groves. The town's primary export is [b]Oak-Gall Tonic[/b], a potent brew made from fermented bark and local herbs. This tonic is a mandatory supply for travelers and military units traversing the Redcliff marshes, as it provides vital resistance to swamp-fevers and fungal spores.

[h2]Key Locations[/h2]
[b]The Bark-Winery[/b]
The industrial heart of the town, where Iron-Oak bark is processed into tonics, medicinal salves, and high-proof local spirits.

[b]The Ceowulf Watchspire[/b]
A tall limestone tower providing early warning for threats emerging from the marshes or approaching from the @[Dwargen](template:8575247c-f649-4462-b757-e4904bf17784) frontiers.

[b]The Drowned Market[/b]
A seasonal trade hub located on the lowest outcroppings. It is a bustling center of commerce that is only accessible during the dry months when the mountain runoff recedes.
