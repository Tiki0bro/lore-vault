Archmagi are powerful wielders of elemental @[Magic](template:2fb7fd89-0e02-4e47-bef5-bf09bcc4e52b). These powerful mages can rearrange the very fabric of reality on a small scale. 

Archmage is a Kingdom title to those who have become a grandmaster in a single school of @[Magic](template:2fb7fd89-0e02-4e47-bef5-bf09bcc4e52b). The title of Archmage is only given to one who has joined the @[Imperium](template:f0b5530f-347c-4467-81fb-3688af0bd6fc) though. Mages and @[Wizards](template:3d9c2749-d0f2-4969-8842-53ff6b0de2e6) that have attained grandmaster status outside of the @[Imperium](template:f0b5530f-347c-4467-81fb-3688af0bd6fc) are called @Renegade Archmagi(Article) these renegades are often feared because they have no alliance with the @[Imperium](template:f0b5530f-347c-4467-81fb-3688af0bd6fc) and are thus considered dangerous. These Archmagi are often hunted down by the @[Imperium](template:f0b5530f-347c-4467-81fb-3688af0bd6fc)'s own Archmagi if a danger to the kingdom or are forced to join the @[Imperium](template:f0b5530f-347c-4467-81fb-3688af0bd6fc), otherwise in some cases they are captured and imprisoned.
