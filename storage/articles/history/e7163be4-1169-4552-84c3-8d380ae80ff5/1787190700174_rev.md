# Overview

The Grave-Stalker is the preeminent biological terror haunting the deep, mineral-rich mines of @[Dwargen](template:8575247c-f649-4462-b757-e4904bf17784). It is a massive, flesh-and-bone apex predator whose body resembles a terrifying fusion of a subterranean reptile and a giant insect, often possessing a monstrous, gorilla-like physique.

# Origin: Mutation by @[The Deepbeat](template:d3db39c4-b2d0-43e2-b147-9d514c1e2215)

Unlike natural fauna or rock-based elementals, the Grave-Stalker is a biological abomination. It is formed through the mutation of subterranean creatures exposed to raw Deepbeat radiation in geologically active zones near the Domain of Mithra. To the Dwarves, they represent the biological embodiment of corrupted flesh and the primal chaos that their disciplined craftsmanship seeks to contain.
