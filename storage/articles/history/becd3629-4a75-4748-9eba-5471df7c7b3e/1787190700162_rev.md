An enchanted fountain that draws water from an underground river. This fountain uses gravitational @[Magic](template:2fb7fd89-0e02-4e47-bef5-bf09bcc4e52b) to force the water upstream out of the fountain, whilst purifying it at the same time.

The fountain is constantly monitored by the Mages who manage the @[Somarth, School of the Arcane](location:0c485ca9-8af8-4950-ba1a-e342d466fda5) not to far from the fountain iself.
