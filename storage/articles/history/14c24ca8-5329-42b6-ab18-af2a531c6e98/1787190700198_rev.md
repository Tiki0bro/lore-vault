[h1]The @[Theurges](template:15f114ad-bf6b-4fe9-8006-399b89226ac6) of the Pulse (The Spirit Guides)[/h1]
The @[Theurges](template:15f114ad-bf6b-4fe9-8006-399b89226ac6) are the intellectual and spiritual core of The Conclave, responsible for divine interpretation, healing, blessings, and moral guidance. They are the primary petitioners to the @[Gods](template:591a5a16-0dc5-4468-aee7-5fdc45ff2055), seeking to channel the @[Godpulse](template:fb360efd-f437-4fb8-996a-1e5e41963a0a) on behalf of the faithful.

[h2]The Two Sacred Orders[/h2]
The @[Theurges](template:15f114ad-bf6b-4fe9-8006-399b89226ac6) are internally divided into two distinct orders, each dedicated to a Greater God.

[h3]The Scepter of @[Ethos](template:01ebc462-0f1e-46bb-87b3-f86e08b87be0): [/h3]These @[Theurges](template:15f114ad-bf6b-4fe9-8006-399b89226ac6) are guided by the doctrine of Perfect Symmetry. They serve as legal advisors and inquisitors, focusing on law, justice, truth, and radiant @[Magic](template:2fb7fd89-0e02-4e47-bef5-bf09bcc4e52b). Their temples are grand and geometrically precise, mirroring their god's view of the @[Godpulse](template:fb360efd-f437-4fb8-996a-1e5e41963a0a).

[h3]The Veil of @[Khemos](template:d834b984-7678-4e4f-b49c-fa9a235a0ddb): [/h3]These @[Theurges](template:15f114ad-bf6b-4fe9-8006-399b89226ac6) are guided by the doctrine of Cyclical Balance. They serve as healers, counselors, and purveyors of mysteries. Their @[Magic](template:2fb7fd89-0e02-4e47-bef5-bf09bcc4e52b) focuses on protection, subtle manipulation, and restorative arts. Their temples, such as the Temple of @[Khemos](template:d834b984-7678-4e4f-b49c-fa9a235a0ddb) Redcliff, are often serene and contemplative spaces.

[h2]Training and Purpose[/h2]
The training of a Theurge is focused on intensive spiritual and academic study of sacred texts, rituals, and the philosophies of their chosen deity. They learn to establish a clear connection to their god through prayer and meditation. @[Theurges](template:15f114ad-bf6b-4fe9-8006-399b89226ac6) of Lesser @[Gods](template:591a5a16-0dc5-4468-aee7-5fdc45ff2055) (Zylos, Laria, etc.) exist but act as subsidiary clergy, answering to the higher-ranking @[Theurges](template:15f114ad-bf6b-4fe9-8006-399b89226ac6) of @[Ethos](template:01ebc462-0f1e-46bb-87b3-f86e08b87be0) and @[Khemos](template:d834b984-7678-4e4f-b49c-fa9a235a0ddb). The @[Theurges](template:15f114ad-bf6b-4fe9-8006-399b89226ac6) of the Pulse are jointly led by an Arch-Theurge of @[Ethos](template:01ebc462-0f1e-46bb-87b3-f86e08b87be0) and an Arch-Theurge of @[Khemos](template:d834b984-7678-4e4f-b49c-fa9a235a0ddb), who form the supreme spiritual authority in the kingdom.
