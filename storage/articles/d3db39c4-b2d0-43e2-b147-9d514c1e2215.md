[h1]The Deepbeat: Dwarven Interpretation of the Godpulse[/h1]
To the Dwarves, the Godpulse is a tangible, physical force of creation known as The Deepbeat. They feel it as sustained pressure and resonance vibrating through the bedrock and metal, a constant, low-frequency hum that they believe holds the memory of the world's core.

[h2]Philosophical Foundation: Permanence and Structure[/h2]
Dwarves believe they were not born, but "struck, like a chord from the mountain," meaning their very existence is tied to the world's structural integrity. Their magic, therefore, is rooted in permanence, endurance, and the material world.

[h2]Magic as Craft:[/h2] The Deepbeat is interpreted as the foundational rhythm of existence. To master it, one must learn to "tune" a material to this resonance. This means magical skill is inseparable from the craft of a smith or mason.

[h2]The Anti-Fleeting:[/h2] Dwarves view the temporary spells of other races (like the Imperium's fleeting Spell Formations) as wasteful and weak. True magic is a permanent or semi-permanent enchantment that locks the material into a state of continuous resonance with the Deepbeat.

[h2]Practical Application:[/h2] Percussive Casting
Dwarven casters use physical force to channel and release the Deepbeat's stored energy.

[h2]The Runesmith: [/h2]This is the most common caster. The Runesmith's hammer and chisel are their arcane foci. They carve intricate runes into metal or stone, adjusting the depth and angle until the material hums with the perfect Deepbeat frequency. The final, powerful strike of the hammer acts as the Casting Strike, releasing the stored resonance to activate the enchantment.

[h2]The Geomancer: [/h2]These casters listen to the "Stone Whispers"—the pressure felt in bone and tooth—to focus on large, structural magic. They use ceremonial war-hammers or striking rods to deliver a potent percussive force to bedrock, reinforcing tunnel walls or sending localized vibrations to cause tremors.

[h2]The Battle Smith: [/h2]A specialist who uses pre-carved, temporary Deep-Runes etched onto their shield or armor. By striking the rune with their hammer in combat, they instantly release a localized effect, such as a burst of force to repel a foe or a momentary field of extreme hardness to resist an incoming blow.

[h2]Cultural and External Relations[/h2]
[h3]Conflict with The Stillness:[/h3] The constant, deep humming of active runes and the pervasive resonance within Dwarven settlements is overwhelming "noise" to Norgn Ice-Seers, making high-level Stillness magic nearly impossible near dwarven activity.

[h3]View of Aristol Memphis:[/h3] Dwarves hold grudging respect for Aristol's Dawn sword, acknowledging his ambition and the economic challenge it presented. However, they view his Words of Power and Spell Formations as structurally inferior, temporary "soft magic" compared to their enduring, physically locked enchantments.
