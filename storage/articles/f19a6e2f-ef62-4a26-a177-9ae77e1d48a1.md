Born from the shadow and resident of the @[Shadow Realm](location:fc24f94d-20e2-4ed5-9b1c-1ec45db12892), she attained her power through seducing the @[First Shadow](person:875055c4-75f7-41a4-a2a9-8fc4ddef0975) and stealing a portion of his power, weakening him in the process, she used her newly attained powers to create offspring. These offspring became known as the @[Aya-la](species:6375f1b0-7638-464a-85ae-413f72d69a45), Which means to come from Aya. She became a prominant figure in the @[Shadow Realm](template:fc24f94d-20e2-4ed5-9b1c-1ec45db12892) with her children the @[Aya-la](template:6375f1b0-7638-464a-85ae-413f72d69a45) inheriting some of her power. She carved out a territory large enough in the @[Shadow Realm](template:fc24f94d-20e2-4ed5-9b1c-1ec45db12892) which became @[The Shaded Isles](location:59587f72-a21c-481f-8d14-0469afd6a25b)
