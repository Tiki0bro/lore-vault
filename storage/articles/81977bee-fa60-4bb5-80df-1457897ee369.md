[h1]The Pantheon of @[Dalari](template:1c118e12-4edb-4600-9e38-402a9a69b304): The @[Gods](template:591a5a16-0dc5-4468-aee7-5fdc45ff2055) and Their Magical Philosophies[/h1]
The @[Gods](template:591a5a16-0dc5-4468-aee7-5fdc45ff2055) of @[Dalari](template:1c118e12-4edb-4600-9e38-402a9a69b304) are the highest wielders of the @[Godpulse](template:fb360efd-f437-4fb8-996a-1e5e41963a0a), the cosmic source of all @[Magic](template:2fb7fd89-0e02-4e47-bef5-bf09bcc4e52b). Each deity, having ascended from mortality by attaining a God Spark, perceives and filters this raw energy according to their divine domain and the collective faith of their followers. Their philosophies create the fundamental laws of @[Magic](template:2fb7fd89-0e02-4e47-bef5-bf09bcc4e52b) for the races who worship them.

[h2]1. The Human Pantheon (@[The Pulse Divine](template:11b07ea1-f4df-4a42-9cd5-fd0f59027a30))[/h2]
These deities anchor the @[Artilian](template:eab4652d-235b-4aa5-b1ae-e592ee8df26f) Church and reflect the human view of the @[Godpulse](template:fb360efd-f437-4fb8-996a-1e5e41963a0a) as @[The Pulse Divine](template:11b07ea1-f4df-4a42-9cd5-fd0f59027a30)—a measurable force of rhythm, structure, and progress. Their clerics wield @[Magic](template:2fb7fd89-0e02-4e47-bef5-bf09bcc4e52b) based on The Aetheric Frequency, focused on control and order.

[h3]The Greater @[Gods](template:591a5a16-0dc5-4468-aee7-5fdc45ff2055) (Balance of Order)[/h3]
[h4]@[Ethos](template:01ebc462-0f1e-46bb-87b3-f86e08b87be0) (Human, Greater God):[/h4] The God of Sun, Light, Order, and Justice. @[Ethos](template:01ebc462-0f1e-46bb-87b3-f86e08b87be0) perceives the @[Godpulse](template:fb360efd-f437-4fb8-996a-1e5e41963a0a) as Perfect Symmetry, justifying the structured, measurable @[Magic](template:2fb7fd89-0e02-4e47-bef5-bf09bcc4e52b) of the @[Imperium](template:f0b5530f-347c-4467-81fb-3688af0bd6fc). He is the patron of law and intellectual rigor.

[h4]@[Khemos](template:d834b984-7678-4e4f-b49c-fa9a235a0ddb) (Human, Greater God): [/h4]The God of Moon, Night, Healing, and Mysteries. @[Khemos](template:d834b984-7678-4e4f-b49c-fa9a235a0ddb) embodies Cyclical Balance, granting @[Magic](template:2fb7fd89-0e02-4e47-bef5-bf09bcc4e52b) that focuses on protection, restoration, and the flow between action and rest. He is the twin of @[Ethos](template:01ebc462-0f1e-46bb-87b3-f86e08b87be0) and the patron of all healers.

[h3]The Lesser @[Gods](template:591a5a16-0dc5-4468-aee7-5fdc45ff2055) (Function and Progress)[/h3]
[h4]Zylos (Human, Lesser God): [/h4]The God of Innovation, @[Magic](template:2fb7fd89-0e02-4e47-bef5-bf09bcc4e52b), Trade, and Wealth. Zylos champions Accelerating Change, granting @[Magic](template:2fb7fd89-0e02-4e47-bef5-bf09bcc4e52b) for functional, repeatable industry. He is the patron of the @[Artilian](template:eab4652d-235b-4aa5-b1ae-e592ee8df26f) Enchanters' Guild and all wizardry.

[h4]Karnak (Human, Lesser God): [/h4]The God of War, Strategy, Courage, and Endurance. Karnak promotes Structured Rhythm and methodical combat, patronizing the military and the @[Silverhawks](template:5dae67da-f394-4571-97e9-3cb8abb2e0a8).

[h4]Laria (Human, Lesser God):[/h4] The Goddess of Bountiful Harvest, Family, and Resilience. Laria embodies Stable Rhythm, granting quiet, protective @[Magic](template:2fb7fd89-0e02-4e47-bef5-bf09bcc4e52b) focused on the continuity of life and lineage.

[h4]Veritas (Human, Lesser God): [/h4]The God of Truth, Clarity, Scrutiny, and Revelation. His @[Magic](template:2fb7fd89-0e02-4e47-bef5-bf09bcc4e52b), The Unveiled Pulse, grants the power to dispel illusions and reveal hidden facts, patronizing judges and inquisitors.

[h4]Phantasus (Human, Lesser God):[/h4] The God of Illusion, Deception, Secrets, and Perception. Phantasus grants @[Magic](template:2fb7fd89-0e02-4e47-bef5-bf09bcc4e52b) that creates temporary falsehoods by manipulating The Altered Frequency.

[h4]Arcanus (Human, Lesser God):[/h4] The God of Architecture, Structure, and Wards. He grants Codified Geometry for creating permanent magical structural integrity in fortresses and buildings.

[h4]Amour (Human, Lesser God):[/h4] The God of Love, Passion, Desire, and Diplomacy. Amour's @[Magic](template:2fb7fd89-0e02-4e47-bef5-bf09bcc4e52b) relies on Emotional Resonance, using powerful sentiment to create creative, social, and binding magical effects.

[h2]2. Pantheons of Order and Element[/h2]
These deities reflect the unique physical and elemental philosophies of the older, non-human races.

[h3]The Elven Pantheon (The @[Lifesong](template:add773c7-4d67-4607-a335-bb94b32a5d19))[/h3]
[h4]Auralis (Elf, Greater God):[/h4] Goddess of Beauty, Form, Artistry, and Harmony. She perceives Perfect Form and Geometry, guiding the artistic manipulation of The @[Lifesong](template:add773c7-4d67-4607-a335-bb94b32a5d19).

[h4]Ildara (Elf, Lesser God):[/h4] Goddess of Wilderness, Transformation, and Seasons. She represents the Chaotic Melody—the necessary fluctuation and wild change within the @[Lifesong](template:add773c7-4d67-4607-a335-bb94b32a5d19).

[h2]The Dwarven Pantheon (@[The Deepbeat](template:d3db39c4-b2d0-43e2-b147-9d514c1e2215))[/h2]
[h4]Ghoran (Dwarf, Greater God): [/h4]God of Endurance, Craftsmanship, and Wealth. He embodies Enduring Resonance, the primary structural force behind @[The Deepbeat](template:d3db39c4-b2d0-43e2-b147-9d514c1e2215).

[h4]Mithra (Dwarf, Lesser God):[/h4] Goddess of Discovery, Secrets, Metal, and Earthquakes. She represents Subterranean Pressure—the hidden pulse of the world's core.

[h4]Vulkos (Dwarf, Lesser God):[/h4] God of Destruction, Metallurgy, and Revolution. He embodies Necessary Destruction, patronizing those who destroy old forms to usher in new creation.

[h2]Other Primal/Elemental @[Gods](template:591a5a16-0dc5-4468-aee7-5fdc45ff2055)[/h2]
[h4]Thalassa (Meranth, Greater God): [/h4]Goddess of The Sea, Tides, Currents, and Memory. She embodies Fluid Motion, the constant, unseen flow of @[The Undertide](template:2a9b7e52-0451-443c-865f-23517e8bd53e).

[h4]Ragnar (Norgn, Lesser God):[/h4] God of Clarity, Patience, Winter, and Fate. He commands Absolute Stillness, the power derived from the absence of motion.

[h4]Ruakahu (Marike, Lesser God):[/h4] God of Wild Growth, Life Cycle, and Resilience. He is the source of The Uhi—the core life force derived from the forest.

[h2]3. @[Gods](template:591a5a16-0dc5-4468-aee7-5fdc45ff2055) of Shadow, Chaos, and Destruction (Evil Domains)[/h2]
These deities harness the corruptive, violent, and chaotic aspects of the @[Godpulse](template:fb360efd-f437-4fb8-996a-1e5e41963a0a).

[h4]The @[Raven Queen](template:f19a6e2f-ef62-4a26-a177-9ae77e1d48a1) (Aya) (Shadow Entity, Greater God):[/h4] Goddess of Death, Shadow, Murder, and Loss. Her philosophy is Stolen Power, derived from the volatile accumulation of energy stolen from the @[First Shadow](template:875055c4-75f7-41a4-a2a9-8fc4ddef0975). She rules the @[Shadow Realm](template:fc24f94d-20e2-4ed5-9b1c-1ec45db12892) and commands the @[Aya-la](template:6375f1b0-7638-464a-85ae-413f72d69a45).

[h4]Kaelos (Orc/Ashen, Greater God): [/h4]God of Rage, Passion, Vitality, and War. He is The Inner Furnace, the source of the chaotic battle-fury inherent in The @[Emberflow](template:681a786d-2f95-4c19-886a-f8c6cb8b2f8a).

[h4]Ashara (Orc/Ashen, Lesser God):[/h4] Goddess of Pain, Scarification, and Endurance. She harnesses Residual Heat, the lasting physical mark of the @[Emberflow](template:681a786d-2f95-4c19-886a-f8c6cb8b2f8a)'s discharge.

[h4]Malcor (Corrupted Mortal, Lesser God):[/h4] God of Disease, Decay, Pestilence, and Famine. He embodies Corrosive Decay, the slow, parasitic draining of @[The Pulse Divine](template:11b07ea1-f4df-4a42-9cd5-fd0f59027a30).

[h4]Xanathus (Corrupted Mortal, Lesser God):[/h4] God of Sacrifice, Betrayal, Obsession, and Vengeance. He represents Fragmented Will, the power of a singular, destructive obsession.

[h2]4. Cosmic Forces (Primal and Independent)[/h2]
These entities exist outside the traditional pantheons, governing fundamental, cosmic laws.

[h4]Chrono (Primordial/Unknown, Greater God):[/h4] God of Time, Space, Destiny, and Preservation. His philosophy is Stable Chronology, representing the unshakeable, linear flow of the universe. Chrono is highly mysterious and focused on preserving the timeline against Chaos Lords.

[h4]The First Light (Primordial Essence, Demi-God):[/h4] The conceptual entity of Creation, Genesis, and the Elemental Core. It embodies the @[Godpulse](template:fb360efd-f437-4fb8-996a-1e5e41963a0a) Essence—the original spark of energy struck when the cosmos began.
