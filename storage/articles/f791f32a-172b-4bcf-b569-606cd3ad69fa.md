Description: An imposing man with a heavy stainless steel prosthetic right hand, he is wearing a long, white cloak. His face is scarred and worn.

Personality: He is extraordinarily eccentric. He is always fidgeting with his prosthetic hand and constantly makes clicking noises with his fingers. 

History: When he was but 4 years old his parents immigrated from west march east to Recliff. As a young child he wandered into the woods despite his mother's warnings against it. Afterwards he claimed he had encountered dire rats who tried and succesfully ate his hand. He had a knack for the arcane arts and eventually travelled to Artilian and joined the imperium, after joining the imperium he was able to research and create for himself a prosthetic hand using magic and enchanting. For many years he taught at the Imperium in Artilian until he was put up for a promotion and became the current Archmage for the @[Somarth, School of the Arcane](location:0c485ca9-8af8-4950-ba1a-e342d466fda5), Without a family of his own to hold him back, he immediately made the journey and was able to relocate to @[Fort Redcliff](location:5a4dd25a-0ef1-4fc1-85b8-8de8b2d884e3).
