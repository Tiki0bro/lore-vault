Sovereigns represent the pinnacle of a Chaos lords evolution. Sovereigns have devoured entire worlds and killed countless Dieties accross the universe. 

Sovereigns can seldom be destroyed as their affinty to the chaos can sustain them with near immortality. 

Sovereigns have intelligence which is gained by devouring countlesss other intelligent life, which makes sovereigns more crafty than their lesser Chaos lords. 

Sovereigns have the power to contend with @Greater @[Gods](template:591a5a16-0dc5-4468-aee7-5fdc45ff2055)(species) with a large advantage, @Lesser @[Gods](template:591a5a16-0dc5-4468-aee7-5fdc45ff2055)(species) are considered cannon fodder in front of a Sovereign

Sovereigns can command any @[Chaos Lord](template:100741c4-0cce-4f3d-a20b-b1f4a7e02164) below its rank into servitude.

Sovereigns have the ability to utilise the powers of chaos in any way shape or form.
