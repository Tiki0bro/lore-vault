Fort Redcliff, what once was a small garrison to guard against the @Dwarves(Species) in @Dwargen(location) has sinced turned into a trading hub of massive potential not shortly after signing a peace treaty with the @Dwarves(species) in the Mountains to the west. This castle thrives thanks to the fertile soil which surrounds its countryside and also the abundance of fish and wild game. Economically the Fort thrives due to having multiple exports and imports flowing through its many ports. Some refer to Fort Redcliff as the nations "Queen" capital of the west[i] (technically south west) [/i]due to both its military presence and its strong positioning amoungst the rivers, with its high stone walls surrounding the city.[img:3618397]

Redcliff's rivers give it life, During the rainy seasons the river rises causing the water to wet the lowlands as a natural form of irrigation bringing life to the crops and fields and making the land fertile. This also brings with it periods of sickness as the land becomes more wet and humid. However the @[Temple of Khemos Redcliff](location:12bf2cd0-109f-44ce-aab1-47fba6798694) fufill their duty as caretakers of the sick and afflicted and provide healing to those truly in need. In Winter the land doesn't snow, it just gets cold and foggy. Spring brings forth a fruitful harvest and Summer brings forth good trade as Redcliff sell off all products in which there is excess.

Redcliff has a diverse population, however being mainly occupied by humans followed by halflings. Being a few days travel between @[Dwargen](landmark:8575247c-f649-4462-b757-e4904bf17784), there are also lots of dwarves who make the journey to red cliff to trade goods and as tourists. With such a diverse population Redcliff has a lot of city guards, whom patrol the city day and night. The city watch is under the direction of @[Semarel](person:ef50c62f-9bc9-4f7a-bffd-66438c454184), the current knight guard captain. It is forbidden to use dangerous evocation magic while in the city walls with the exception of the @[Somarth, School of the Arcane](location:0c485ca9-8af8-4950-ba1a-e342d466fda5) which has the correct facilities and precautions. Breaking this law results in immediate incarceration and in serious cases death if the accused is found guilty during the trial.
