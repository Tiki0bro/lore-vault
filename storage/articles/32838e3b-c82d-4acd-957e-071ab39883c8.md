[h1]The Primal Breath: Dragon Magic of Scale and Time[/h1]
Dragons perceive the Godpulse as The Primal Breath—the slow, deep, life-sustaining expansion and contraction of the universe . Their magic is tied to elemental totality and time, giving them power that scales with their millennia-long existence.

[h2]Core Philosophy: Accumulation and Expression[/h2]
Dragon magic is an innate, continuous process of elemental accumulation that scales exponentially with age.

[h2]The World is a Hoard:[/h2] Dragons interpret the Godpulse as a massive, cosmic hoard of pure elemental energy. The more energy they accumulate and integrate into their being, the closer they are to mastering the Primal Breath. Their physical hoards are simply a reflection of this magical philosophy.

[h2]Magic as Innate Being: [/h2]Dragon magic is not cast through formula (Humans) or resonance (Dwarves); it is expressed through their immense physical form. Their power is stable, ancient elemental order, which is the perfect counter to the formless, destructive Chaos Magic of the Sovereigns.

[h2]Stages of Power (Accumulation)[/h2]
A dragon's magical power is directly proportional to its age, as every moment of its existence is an act of integrating elemental energy.

[h1]Dragon Stage[/h1]

[h2]Young/Lesser[/h2]
[h3]Elemental Potential[/h3]

Power is localized and volatile. They rely on the basic Elemental Exhale (breath weapon), a violent discharge of recently consumed elemental energy. Not a match for Greater Gods.

[h2]Greater[/h2]
[h3]Elemental Integration[/h3]

Power stabilizes and is integrated into their form (e.g., scales, blood). They subconsciously perform the Primal Inhale (Absorption), constantly drawing in and purifying elemental particles from the world.

[h2]Elder[/h2]
[h3]Cosmic Rivalry[/h3]

The pinnacle of power, rivaling Greater Gods and Sovereign Chaos Lords. Their massive accumulation allows them to temporarily overwrite local elemental laws.

[h2]Practical Techniques[/h2]
Dragon magic is characterized by its scale, control, and stability.

[h2]The Elemental Exhale[/h2] (Cataclysmic Casting): The dragon's breath weapon is their signature spell, but at the Elder level, it is a cataclysmic event. It is a massive, localized projection of elemental totality that temporarily overwrites the local elemental rules of the Material Realm.

[h2]Hoard Transference[/h2] (Geological Enchantment): The physical hoard is a gigantic, long-term magical focus. By continuously Exhaling the Primal Breath over their treasure for centuries, they saturate it with raw, stable elemental magic, creating artifacts of immense power through time and proximity.

[h2]Primal Shout (Spatial Disruption): [/h2]An Elder Dragon's ancient roar is a focused Exhale that can momentarily rupture the local "texture" of the Godpulse. This destabilizes the Aetheric Frequency for miles, causing human Words of Power and Spell Formations to fail catastrophically in their vicinity.
