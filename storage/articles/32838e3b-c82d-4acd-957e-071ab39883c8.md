[h1]The Primal Breath: Dragon @[Magic](template:2fb7fd89-0e02-4e47-bef5-bf09bcc4e52b) of Scale and Time[/h1]
Dragons perceive the @[Godpulse](template:fb360efd-f437-4fb8-996a-1e5e41963a0a) as The Primal Breath—the slow, deep, life-sustaining expansion and contraction of the universe . Their @[Magic](template:2fb7fd89-0e02-4e47-bef5-bf09bcc4e52b) is tied to elemental totality and time, giving them power that scales with their millennia-long existence.

[h2]Core Philosophy: Accumulation and Expression[/h2]
Dragon @[Magic](template:2fb7fd89-0e02-4e47-bef5-bf09bcc4e52b) is an innate, continuous process of elemental accumulation that scales exponentially with age.

[h2]The World is a Hoard:[/h2] Dragons interpret the @[Godpulse](template:fb360efd-f437-4fb8-996a-1e5e41963a0a) as a massive, cosmic hoard of pure elemental energy. The more energy they accumulate and integrate into their being, the closer they are to mastering the Primal Breath. Their physical hoards are simply a reflection of this magical philosophy.

[h2]@[Magic](template:2fb7fd89-0e02-4e47-bef5-bf09bcc4e52b) as Innate Being: [/h2]Dragon @[Magic](template:2fb7fd89-0e02-4e47-bef5-bf09bcc4e52b) is not cast through formula (Humans) or resonance (Dwarves); it is expressed through their immense physical form. Their power is stable, ancient elemental order, which is the perfect counter to the formless, destructive Chaos @[Magic](template:2fb7fd89-0e02-4e47-bef5-bf09bcc4e52b) of the Sovereigns.

[h2]Stages of Power (Accumulation)[/h2]
A dragon's magical power is directly proportional to its age, as every moment of its existence is an act of integrating elemental energy.

[h1]Dragon Stage[/h1]

[h2]Young/Lesser[/h2]
[h3]Elemental Potential[/h3]

Power is localized and volatile. They rely on the basic Elemental Exhale (breath weapon), a violent discharge of recently consumed elemental energy. Not a match for Greater @[Gods](template:591a5a16-0dc5-4468-aee7-5fdc45ff2055).

[h2]Greater[/h2]
[h3]Elemental Integration[/h3]

Power stabilizes and is integrated into their form (e.g., scales, blood). They subconsciously perform the Primal Inhale (Absorption), constantly drawing in and purifying elemental particles from the world.

[h2]Elder[/h2]
[h3]Cosmic Rivalry[/h3]

The pinnacle of power, rivaling Greater @[Gods](template:591a5a16-0dc5-4468-aee7-5fdc45ff2055) and @[Sovereign](template:28a43fd7-9ad6-4215-ae90-fd026e1951f4) Chaos Lords. Their massive accumulation allows them to temporarily overwrite local elemental laws.

[h2]Practical Techniques[/h2]
Dragon @[Magic](template:2fb7fd89-0e02-4e47-bef5-bf09bcc4e52b) is characterized by its scale, control, and stability.

[h2]The Elemental Exhale[/h2] (Cataclysmic Casting): The dragon's breath weapon is their signature spell, but at the Elder level, it is a cataclysmic event. It is a massive, localized projection of elemental totality that temporarily overwrites the local elemental rules of the Material Realm.

[h2]Hoard Transference[/h2] (Geological Enchantment): The physical hoard is a gigantic, long-term magical focus. By continuously Exhaling the Primal Breath over their treasure for centuries, they saturate it with raw, stable elemental @[Magic](template:2fb7fd89-0e02-4e47-bef5-bf09bcc4e52b), creating artifacts of immense power through time and proximity.

[h2]Primal Shout (Spatial Disruption): [/h2]An Elder Dragon's ancient roar is a focused Exhale that can momentarily rupture the local "texture" of the @[Godpulse](template:fb360efd-f437-4fb8-996a-1e5e41963a0a). This destabilizes the Aetheric Frequency for miles, causing human @[Words of Power](template:4db351c9-18ff-41db-a6d9-6887aa490947) and @[Spell Formations](template:762bed00-b3d7-4f2d-ba07-4d8275605066) to fail catastrophically in their vicinity.
