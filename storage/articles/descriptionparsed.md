Dalari is a world that came into existence when a powerful entity decided to split a world called <b>Earth</b> in two. One world becoming a world of Magic and Gods and the other becoming a world with advanced technology. Although these worlds were separated by billions of lightyears away they are still two worlds that are linked through magic. 
<span class="line-spacer d-block">&nbsp;</span>
Dalari is the largest continent in this world and typically thought of by its residents as the entirety of the world of Dalari, however there exists continents towards the south, the continent of Murr which is a smaller and unknown to the majority of the races that dwell on Dalari. These two continents are separted by a vast and unforgiving ocean and with the current technology these seas are inpossible to navigate, not only the storms that rage, but also the monsters that live deep within these oceans. Therefore the two continents have remained isolated from each other each developing at a different rate than the other. 
<span class="line-spacer d-block">&nbsp;</span>
Dalari is a world of Magic, Swords and Socerery and most importantly it is a world of Gods. The Gods of dalari live on Dalari but are secluded in their pocket dimensions or there own divine demi-planes. Some gods are friendly and guide the races of Dalari towards a brighter future and some like to cause mischief and pain for its inhabitants and there are lots who just don&#039;t care and spend their days doing only what they feel like doing. You will find the worship of gods is the norm and part of everyday life, however there are just as many people who revile the gods in this world.

There are many races that live on the world of Dalari. We have humankind which have created an empire known as the &quot;Artilian Kingdom&quot; which is a collection of city states under the rule of a single king. There are also a group of human city states on the west coast that are called the &quot;Free Cities&quot; which are a loosely allied group of cities that govern themselves and have no ties to the &quot;Artilian Kingdom&quot; other than trading. The Fridged north is home to 3 independent Norgn Kingdoms each kingdom vying over the lands and riches that the other posseses, These 3 Kingdoms have been hostile towards eachother since before the &quot;Chaos wars&quot;. Dwarves live within the mountains throughout Dalari with the greatest of the cities being the Dwarven capital of Dwargen. The Dwarves outside of Dwargen are all independent dwarven kingdoms in there own regards. In the &quot;Blasted Plains&quot; various races have allied themselves together to form an Alliance called the &quot;Ashen League&quot; a group comprised of orcs, hobgoblins, goblins and ogres, unfriendly towards outsiders. The &quot;Ashen League&quot; often have skirmishes with humans who tress pass on their land.
