The Human capital city of Artilian rests at the very heart of @[Dalari](template:1c118e12-4edb-4600-9e38-402a9a69b304). The city is situated on the north side of the @Shenola Isle(location). Artilian refers to not only to the city, but is also the very name of the Kingdom in which it belongs. The city of Artilian is the 2nd largest city in the @Artilian Kingdom(Location) coming second only to its brother city @@[Bastillian](template:fdc1e8c2-d28b-49ed-82d9-225bdbe7e7b3)(location) which is north of Artilian along the border of the @[Chaos Lands](location:1c5cc255-34fd-4863-8158-040ec5a050de), however unlike its brother Artilian has spared no expense in making its capital extravegant and beautiful. With its large spires and elven and dwarven inspired architecture, the city stands proudly as a testament to the innovations and hardwork of the human race. 

Artilian has one of the most effective and innovative waste management system in the Kingdom which has led to a much lower rate of disease and sickness within its city, resulting in a much higher life expectancy for its residents and a happier quality of life.

Artilian despite being the human capital has a very diverse demographic, with the majority being humans, there are races of all shapes and sizes, this has resulted in a very mixed human lineage. However unlike the @Elves(species) which value bloodline purity, humans have embraced the diversity that has come as a natural result of interracial marriages. Outside of the capital however, th smaller towns and villages may still harbor some prejudices to non human races especially towards the likes of @Orcs(species) and @[Goblinkind](species:ad3ed6da-9b79-4b12-bdc8-c2392362b398) as there are still lots of tribes that are hostile towards humans, so prejudice is found throughout the human realm.
