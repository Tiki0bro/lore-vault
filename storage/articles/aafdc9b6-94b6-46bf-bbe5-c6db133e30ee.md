[h1]The Stillness[/h1]
To the @[url=article:a30a5bc4-265c-432b-8dbe-e170cdf27ff2]Norgn[/url], the inhabitants of the frozen north, the @[url=article:fb360efd-f437-4fb8-996a-1e5e41963a0a]@[Godpulse](template:fb360efd-f437-4fb8-996a-1e5e41963a0a)[/url] is not a sound, a rhythm, or a flow, but an absence. They call it [b]The Stillness[/b]—the silent tension between breaths, the frozen, unchanging moment that precedes all motion and chaos. It is the belief that true power is found in the latent potential of the universe, and only a perfectly quiet mind can perceive this truth.

[h2]I. Magical Philosophy: Revelation, Not Creation[/h2]
@[The Norgn](template:a30a5bc4-265c-432b-8dbe-e170cdf27ff2) believe that all magical phenomena—a wall of ice, a blinding snowstorm, a vision of the future—already exist as possibilities within the Aetheric Frequency. Their @[Magic](template:2fb7fd89-0e02-4e47-bef5-bf09bcc4e52b) is fundamentally about [i]revelation[/i] rather than creation. To a Norgn, a spell is not \"cast\"; it is [b]uncovered[/b] from the existing fabric of reality.

[b]The Power of Clarity:[/b] Wielding The Stillness requires absolute disciplined clarity. @[The Norgn](template:a30a5bc4-265c-432b-8dbe-e170cdf27ff2) must filter out the \"noise\" of fleeting desires, fears, and immediate sensations to perceive the deep, latent truths of the @[Godpulse](template:fb360efd-f437-4fb8-996a-1e5e41963a0a). This clarity is sustained and anchored by their strong familial bonds, as trust and collective calm help to stabilize the caster's focus.

[h2]II. Manifestation and The Silent Art[/h2]
When an Ice-Seer manifests a spell, there are no @[Words of Power](template:4db351c9-18ff-41db-a6d9-6887aa490947), dramatic gestures, or glowing @[Runes](template:22a74c4e-99e3-4103-9d1e-b2d9f225c901). The process is entirely internal and nearly instantaneous.

[b]The Silent Manifestation:[/b] The caster achieves a state of perfect internal stillness and projects the \"idea\" of the magical effect onto the environment. The desired effect (e.g., solidifying a section of a lake or freezing the air in an opponent's lungs) manifests as the caster forces the latent elemental particles to conform to a state of stasis.

[h2]III. The Ice-Seers[/h2]
The specialized practitioners of The Stillness are known as [b]Ice-Seers[/b]. Their training involves years of meditation in the thinnest air of the northern crags, learning to synchronize their heartbeat with the slow, tectonic vibrations of the earth.

[b]Defensive Mastery:[/b] Their @[Magic](template:2fb7fd89-0e02-4e47-bef5-bf09bcc4e52b) is devastating as a counter or defense. When faced with an attack, an Ice-Seer achieves immediate clarity, allowing them to reveal perfectly placed defenses. A common feat is freezing the very moisture in the air around an incoming arrow, turning it into a brittle shard of ice that shatters before it can strike.

[b]Divination and Foresight:[/b] The pursuit of silence allows Norgn seers to perceive faint, latent information within the @[Godpulse](template:fb360efd-f437-4fb8-996a-1e5e41963a0a). They can \"see\" future events that have yet to gain enough energy to become \"noise,\" making them highly valued for foresight across @[Dalari](template:1c118e12-4edb-4600-9e38-402a9a69b304).

[h2]IV. The Great Monoliths of Vorstgrad[/h2]
Located in the ancestral home of @[The Norgn](template:a30a5bc4-265c-432b-8dbe-e170cdf27ff2), the [b]Great Monoliths[/b] are massive stone structures that hum with the very first notes of the Stillness. 

[b]Resonant Anchors:[/b] These monoliths are believed to be the primary anchors for The Stillness in the physical world. Ice-Seers often travel to Vorstgrad to \"retune\" their internal clarity against the monoliths' perfect, unchanging frequency. It is said that within the circle of the monoliths, even the wind ceases to make a sound.

[h2]V. Vulnerabilities: The Sound of Chaos[/h2]
The effectiveness of The Stillness is highly dependent on environment and mental state.

[b]Dissonance and Noise:[/b] The Stillness is severely disrupted by environments rich in magical \"noise.\" The constant humming of @[url=article:d3db39c4-b2d0-43e2-b147-9d514c1e2215]@[The Deepbeat](template:d3db39c4-b2d0-43e2-b147-9d514c1e2215)[/url] or the chaotic discharge of @[url=article:681a786d-2f95-4c19-886a-f8c6cb8b2f8a]@[Emberflow](template:681a786d-2f95-4c19-886a-f8c6cb8b2f8a)[/url] acts as magical static, making complex casting impossible.

[b]The Danger of Stasis:[/b] The search for ultimate stillness carries the risk of the caster becoming lost. The deepest forms of meditation can lead to physical stasis, where the Ice-Seer becomes permanently frozen—a silent monument to their pursuit of the absolute.
