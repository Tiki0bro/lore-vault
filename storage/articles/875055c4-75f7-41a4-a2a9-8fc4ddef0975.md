An entity born from the @[Shadow Realm](location:fc24f94d-20e2-4ed5-9b1c-1ec45db12892)'s @[Realmcore](item:20a05482-11fd-4953-ab6a-176b7bf3de8b) the first intelligent life to be born in the @[Shadow Realm](location:fc24f94d-20e2-4ed5-9b1c-1ec45db12892), He has existed since the birth of the @[Shadow Realm](template:fc24f94d-20e2-4ed5-9b1c-1ec45db12892) and held unfathomable power, however he was seduced and fell in love with Aya (@[Raven Queen](person:f19a6e2f-ef62-4a26-a177-9ae77e1d48a1)) until one night she stole a portion of his power and in the process tearing a piece of his soul. He was betrayed by the @[Raven Queen](template:f19a6e2f-ef62-4a26-a177-9ae77e1d48a1) and was unable to regain his former power. His soul had been torn and his heart shattered by her betrayal. With an incomplete soul he had fallen from his position as Lord of Shadow  he was challenged by the other Lords of Shadow and his power proved insufficient for him to maintain his position as First Lord. After many defeats he sunk into despair and severe depression and hid himself from those seeking to challenge him. The First Shadow became a recluse, however he still roams the @[Shadow Realm](template:fc24f94d-20e2-4ed5-9b1c-1ec45db12892) always searching for a way to get stronger. 

After many years of searching, the First Shadow was able to make contact with creatures of the Material Realm, although he wanted to travel and aquire power from the Material plane, his link to the @[Realmcore](item:20a05482-11fd-4953-ab6a-176b7bf3de8b) renders him unable to leave the @[Shadow Realm](location:fc24f94d-20e2-4ed5-9b1c-1ec45db12892). Unlike the other shadow creatures and mortals from the Material Realm who can freely move between the two. He then began searching for a way to bypass this limitation. He found that he could make contact with mortals and began searching for vulnerable mortals who were in desperate need of power. He would make a deal with those in need, promising them great power in return for servitude. He promised he could teach them and grant them power over the shadows and other @[Magic](template:2fb7fd89-0e02-4e47-bef5-bf09bcc4e52b) that would make his contractors powerful in the Material Realm. He would do this in exchange for elemental crystals and other powerful @[Magic](template:2fb7fd89-0e02-4e47-bef5-bf09bcc4e52b) items that could help him heal his soul. 

The First Shadow is biding his time in order to regain enough strength to destroy the @[Raven Queen](person:f19a6e2f-ef62-4a26-a177-9ae77e1d48a1) and her people in order to regain his missing power.
