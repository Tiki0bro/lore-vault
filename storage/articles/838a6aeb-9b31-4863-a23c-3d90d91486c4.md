# Overview

Thalenis Vane is a prominent figure in Luminaris, serving as the Grand Chanter of the Resonant Spire and a Voice of the Chorus. As the Overseer of the Lumin (Life-Weavers Guild), he is responsible for the structural and spiritual harmony of the city.

# Character Profile & Background

As a Grand Chanter, Thalenis is one of the few who can commune directly with the Sky-Pillars—the colossal, magically imbued trees that form the foundation of Luminaris. While other mages focus on the visual beauty of the sunstone, Thalenis focuses on the Whispering Harmonics that keep the city's organic structures from decaying.

### Master of the Lifesong
He uses melodic spells to guide the growth of buildings, ensuring they follow the sacred geometry of the Goddess Auralis.

### The Auralian Traditionalist
He is a strict enforcer of Elven conduct. He believes that "You can close your eyes to the light, but you cannot hide your heart from the song."

### Political Standing
He holds a seat on the Chorus of the Houses, often acting as the bridge between the ancient traditions of Eldengard and the modern needs of the Dalari Delegation on Earth.

# Secrets & Conflict
Thalenis is currently troubled by a "hiss" or "discordant note" he has detected deep within the city's roots—a secret he has yet to reveal to the rest of the Chorus.
