[h1]The Thal-Kora[/h1]
The [b]Thal-Kora[/b] (The Dual-Chant) is an ancient Elven discipline of druidic beast-binding centered in [url=https://www.worldanvil.com/w/dalari-tiki0bro/a/aether-s-perch3A-the-sky-crest-eyrie-settlement]Aether's Perch[/url]. While most Elves use the @[Lifesong](template:add773c7-4d67-4607-a335-bb94b32a5d19) to guide the growth of trees or heal the wounded, the Thal-Kora focus their harmonics on the synchronization of consciousness between Elf and beast.

[h2]The Great Harmonics[/h2]
The Thal-Kora identify two primary elemental frequencies used to bridge the gap between Elven spirits and the primal predators of the world. As we discover more creatures, new harmonics may be revealed.

[h3]The Breath of the Silver Sky[/h3]
This crystalline, high-frequency harmonic is used to bond with apex aerial predators like the @[Aether-Hawk](species:d76ed8fb-3786-46da-8ddf-4587d071e3e9). It requires the singer to vibrate their upper throat, mimicking the high-altitude gales. It provides the rider with [b]Current-Sight[/b] and incredible cognitive speed.

[h3]The Anthem of the Mountain’s Root[/h3]
This guttural, chest-heavy harmonic is used to bond with heavy terrestrial predators like the @[Crested Prowler](species:29d29782-890a-473c-b467-4d5aaba3f0e7). It pushes the @[Lifesong](template:add773c7-4d67-4607-a335-bb94b32a5d19) to its lowest register to catch the physical tremors of @[The Deepbeat](template:d3db39c4-b2d0-43e2-b147-9d514c1e2215). It provides the rider with [b]Grounding[/b] and massive physical momentum.

[h2]The Bond-Mandate[/h2]
The Thal-Kora strictly follow the law of [i]"One Soul, One Song."[/i] An Elf chooses their harmonic early in their training. To attempt to hold both the Breath and the Anthem simultaneously is considered a reckless defiance of the @[Godpulse](template:fb360efd-f437-4fb8-996a-1e5e41963a0a), known only to @[Legends](template:b93159cd-a7f7-4469-8ebb-b73bd98d9011) and those who have lost their minds to the dissonance.

[h2]III. Cultural Rituals & Festivals[/h2]

[b]The Rite of the First Chord[/b]
Every member of the Thal-Kora begins their journey with this high-stakes ritual. Initiates are taken to the [i]Threshold Peaks[/i], unarmed and alone. They must meditate until they can hear the @[Godpulse](template:fb360efd-f437-4fb8-996a-1e5e41963a0a) frequency of a specific wild beast. The ritual is completed only when the Elf strikes a harmonic that perfectly matches the animal's spirit, compelling the beast to approach and bow. It is a moment of mutual recognition; if the Elf’s pitch is false or their intent is impure, the beast will simply ignore them—or, in rare cases, attack.

[b]The Festival of the Silver Gale[/b]
Held during the autumnal equinox when the mountain winds reach their peak velocity, this is the most vibrant celebration in Aether's Perch. The Cantors open every resonant vent in the [url=https://www.worldanvil.com/w/dalari-tiki0bro/a/aether-s-perch3A-the-sky-crest-eyrie-settlement]Wind-Harp Spires[/url], turning the entire town into a thundering orchestral instrument. The highlight is the [i]Chorus of Flight[/i], where hundreds of Aether-Hawks spiral into the clouds, their bioluminescent wings flickering in rhythmic patterns dictated by the music of the Spires.

[b]The Vigil of the Tectonic Bass[/b]
A solemn, three-day period of silence observed by the binders of the @[Crested Prowler](species:29d29782-890a-473c-b467-4d5aaba3f0e7). Riders descend into the [i]Sub-Root Caverns[/i] where the mountain's vibrations are strongest. By pressing their palms to the stone and ceasing all speech, they "Deep-Listen" to the world's internal rhythms. This vigil is said to cleanse the rider of arrogance and reconnect them to the grounding power of the [b]Deepbeat[/b], ensuring their bond remains anchored and stable.
