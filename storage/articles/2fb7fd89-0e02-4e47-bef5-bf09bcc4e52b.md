Magic in @[Dalari](template:1c118e12-4edb-4600-9e38-402a9a69b304) is a blanket term for the various powers that exist in this world. The source of all magic is a cosmic force known as the @[Godpulse](template:fb360efd-f437-4fb8-996a-1e5e41963a0a), which is perceived and expressed differently by each race and lineage. While all magic is derived from this one source, it is categorized into three main branches: Elemental, Bloodline, and Divine. Each branch is further broken down into several different types of magic.

[img:3673389]
[b]Elemental Magic. [/b]

Elemental Magic is the utilization of a ubiquitous, omnipresent energy that exists in and around all things. Practitioners can create, control, and manipulate natural phenomena by perceiving and attuning to this energy. While the magical energy is a single source (the @@[Godpulse](template:fb360efd-f437-4fb8-996a-1e5e41963a0a)(article)), different cultures perceive it in unique ways, which in turn shapes their magical practices. [br]
[br]
The Elves hear it as the @@[Lifesong](template:add773c7-4d67-4607-a335-bb94b32a5d19)(article), a harmony they join through spell-singing . [br]
The Dwarves feel it as @@[The Deepbeat](template:d3db39c4-b2d0-43e2-b147-9d514c1e2215)(article), a resonance they channel through rune-forging . [br]
The Orcs and Ashen League feel it as the fiery @@[Emberflow](template:681a786d-2f95-4c19-886a-f8c6cb8b2f8a)(article), which they release through war-chants .[br]
@[The Norgn](template:a30a5bc4-265c-432b-8dbe-e170cdf27ff2) sense it as @@[The Stillness](template:aafdc9b6-94b6-46bf-bbe5-c6db133e30ee)(article), a frozen potential they reveal through meditation .

Elemental magic requires the user to attune to different elemental particles. These particles can be manipulated through @[Words of Power](article:4db351c9-18ff-41db-a6d9-6887aa490947), magical @@[Runes](template:22a74c4e-99e3-4103-9d1e-b2d9f225c901)(article) and @@[Spell Formations](template:762bed00-b3d7-4f2d-ba07-4d8275605066)(article) . Practitioners are typically called @Mages(article) or @@[Wizards](template:3d9c2749-d0f2-4969-8842-53ff6b0de2e6)(Article) . However, other names exist, such as @@[Druids](template:4f6d7507-ba43-4a63-9d83-278dc1dbfcaa)(article), who are mages more in tune with natural and chaotic elemental particles . In theory, magic can be taught to everyone, but some simply don't have a "knack for it" .

[b]Bloodline Magic.[/b]

Bloodline Magic is a type of magic inherited genetically within a family or bloodline . It allows the user to tap into innate magical abilities specific to their lineage . This power is known as 


@[The Inheritance](template:8c21bdd0-c091-4267-9958-053d833999c7), a divine residue passed down through generations that manifests as an awakening of ancestral memory .


Bloodline Magic is different from elemental magic because it draws power from the user's own bloodline, not from external elements . The abilities are unique to each bloodline and can manifest in various forms, such as telekinesis, mind reading, and shapeshifting. This magic is often instinctual and is not based on traditional study or ritual, and it can be both a blessing and a curse due to its drawbacks and limitations .

[img:3673395]
@@[Divine Magic](template:ff44f881-61f4-470e-943e-eeafaddd5962)(article).

@[Divine Magic](template:ff44f881-61f4-470e-943e-eeafaddd5962) draws its power from the deities and their domains, with the @[Gods](template:591a5a16-0dc5-4468-aee7-5fdc45ff2055) themselves perceiving the @[Godpulse](template:fb360efd-f437-4fb8-996a-1e5e41963a0a) as a "living equation of perfect symmetry and motion" . Practitioners of this magic, known as clerics or priests, must have a strong connection to a deity to access its power . @[Divine Magic](template:ff44f881-61f4-470e-943e-eeafaddd5962) is used for purposes ranging from healing to combat . To cast @[Divine Magic](template:ff44f881-61f4-470e-943e-eeafaddd5962), a cleric or priest prays, meditates, or performs a religious ritual to establish a connection with their deity . The strength of their magic depends on their level of devotion and their relationship with their god . This differs from other forms of magic in that it is an act of devotion rather than a physical or inherited ability .
