Beings formed within the chaos of space, born outside the fabric of the mortal realm, formed from chaos they are born formless and with an automatic affinty towards chaos @[Magic](template:2fb7fd89-0e02-4e47-bef5-bf09bcc4e52b), chaos lords use the chaotic energy to create for themselves a vessal or body in order to navigate the various physical realms, chaos lords vary in power, however they grow stronger by destroying and absorbing life, Chaos lords represent an existential threat to the inhabitants of @[Dalari](template:1c118e12-4edb-4600-9e38-402a9a69b304) and all worlds they come accross. 

Chaos lords have their own power system and are ranked accordingly by how much power they posess. With the greatest among them being called @[Sovereign](species:28a43fd7-9ad6-4215-ae90-fd026e1951f4) these sovereigns are capable of contending against multiple @Greater @[Gods](template:591a5a16-0dc5-4468-aee7-5fdc45ff2055)(species) without a disadvantage.

Chaos lords are the scourage of all life and are so destructive and unreasonable that they have no cohesive relationship with anyone or anything, including other Chaos lords. They will however exert dominance over Chaos lords at the lower ranks being able to command them to do their bidding.
