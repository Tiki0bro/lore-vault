Title given to the current Lord of Redcliff. 

This title is bestowed by the king and the one who bears this title will become the @[Sovereign](template:28a43fd7-9ad6-4215-ae90-fd026e1951f4) ruler of the Duchy of Redcliff. @[The House of Ceowulf](template:db4c831a-634f-4ab2-8f0c-95a0072a267f) has been the sole family responsible for the Duchy of Redcliff, due to both thier loyalty to the crown and competence at managing its lands. If for whatever the @King(person) decides to revoke the title, It may also be bestowed to another, however @[The House of Ceowulf](template:db4c831a-634f-4ab2-8f0c-95a0072a267f) has not given the @King(person) any reason to do so.
