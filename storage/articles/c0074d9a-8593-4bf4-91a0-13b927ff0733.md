Deep within the @[Altharian Forest](template:703b8466-a52b-4ce3-abf4-71d28869ff04) lives the a village of humans known as the “Marike O Here” or the “Marike” (Ma-ree-keh-Or-He-reh) which in their native translates into common as the “Children of the forest”. The Marike have resided in the Altahrian forest for centuries and even according to Elven lore keepers the Marike were here even before the elves had arrived in the @[Altharian Forest](template:703b8466-a52b-4ce3-abf4-71d28869ff04). Despite residing in the forest for so long there has been no sign or evidence of expansion, the Marike only having carved out a small area of forest to call their territory. They have built their homes amongst the trees with their dwellings situated well above the forest floor. Their village is built in way the represents their status amongst the Marike with the “Aiki” (Eye-key) or Chief living in highest dwelling way up high in the trees a dwelling equal to his status as overseer of all others. Although there is a Patriarchy with the leaders of the village all being men the traditions of the Marike teach that all are equal in the eyes of the forest. Therefore those who are in leadership positions are called to lead the people but are also expected to serve others and lighten another’s burden, therefore no one is held as superior over another. The “Ia Hine” (E-Yah-He-neh) or Woman are traditionally the leaders of their homes, this means that they serve the men in the home but also are the matriarchs of the home with the men being subservient to them. With all things being equal in the forest the Marike only take from the forest what is needed. They hunt when it is needed but only when it is needed, if they hunt an animal for consumption, they only take what their family would eat for the that night, leaving the rest of the carcass to be eaten and disposed of by the other forest creatures. The Marike treat their prey as brothers and sisters, thanking each animal they hunt for providing them with the sustenance and nutrients to live another day.

The Marike have a life span of 90 – 120 years of age being, however they are considered to have reached adulthood at the age of 16. The Marike have dark brown skin and dark black hair with little variation in color, they have natural green eyes with brown eyes being considered rare the Marike males and females and are around 6-7ft tall with an adult male weighing at around 120kg’s on average they have a solid and sturdy build with the females tending to be taller and more slender however both the males and females are freakishly nimble despite their larger frame, they can maneuver through the forest with the gracefulness of an elf. Both male and female are covered with tribal tattoo’s These markings can mean a variety of things with tattoo’s recounting the lineage of a descendant some tattoo’s depict special events or tattoo’s depict special merits that an individual has achieved. These tattoos are only carved by the villages chief who is the villages spiritual leader. When a Marike reach the age of adulthood (16) a special tattoo is bestowed upon them and carved using a special crystal known as “Uho” (ew-hor) which roughly translates as the “Core” this crystal is known by Elven lore keepers as the “Core of the forest” a special type of nature elemental crystal that are rarely seen in the outside world. The only known location where these crystals frequently appear is within the territory of the Marike. This crystal is ground up into a powder and mixed with tree sap to create a paint like substance this is then embedded into the skin using a handmade needle made completely from Uho and this is called an “Uhi” (ew-he) this causes the powers of the elemental powers within the crystal to fuse into the skin giving power to those that bear the tattoo. These powers manifest in many ways with each person receiving a different power in relation to the natural world around them. 

When a Marike dies, the village will seek out a tree that is dying and will carve out a space within that tree and place the body inside and cover it with the bark of a healthy tree before letting the body rest. After about a week the elemental power from the tattoo will condense within the heart of the dying body creating an Uho. This Uho then gives life to the dying tree, causing it to grow healthy and strong. After many years when the tree has matured it will begin to supply elemental energy to the Uho inside it. When the Uho becomes to big inside the tree it begins to crack and breaks into smaller crystals, these crystals then travel through the roots and a distributed into the soil and earth, the Uho then begins its new life and begins to grow underground and continue to grow until its crystals reach the surface and are found by the Marike or other forest dwellers which could take many years. Even the Marike are unaware of this natural phenomenon although some Chiefs have theorized this in the past.

The Marike are not hospitable to outsiders, especially the Elves with whom the wider forest is shared. The Marike do not have a concept of borders and have no knowledge of where their lands end and where the lands of the elves begin this is why they often come across wandering elves while out hunting with only a few incidents that have resulted in violence with the Marike coming out as the victor in most cases. The elves have tried to make contact with the Marike even getting as far as  personally setting foot in the village of the Marike only to be rejected by the Chief. Although the elves are wary of these Marike the elves have decided that they are not a threat and has decided to leave them be. The Marike also do not view the elves as a threat, but only out of ignorance. 
The @[Altharian Forest](template:703b8466-a52b-4ce3-abf4-71d28869ff04) contains many dangerous and powerful creatures and although the Marike a physically strong and powerful they have a strong link to nature @[Magic](template:2fb7fd89-0e02-4e47-bef5-bf09bcc4e52b), being able to use the energy around them to cast druidic @[Magic](template:2fb7fd89-0e02-4e47-bef5-bf09bcc4e52b). Now according to Marike @[Legends](template:b93159cd-a7f7-4469-8ebb-b73bd98d9011) when the god of the forest “Ruakahu” (Ru-ah-car-who) created the forest and its creatures he began to feel lonely because he was the only one of his kind and in order to stave off the feeling of loneliness, he carved the Marike out of a tree and breathed life into them bringing about the birth of the Marike. Because of this, when the Marike a threatened they can use the power of the Uho which condenses and crystalizes within their heart which pumps the crystals throughout the body the Uho in turn morphs their bodies into a body of a tree, reverting back to their original form which to the Marike is in accordance to their @[Legends](template:b93159cd-a7f7-4469-8ebb-b73bd98d9011). This transformation is very similar to the spell “barkskin” which is used by @[Wizards](template:3d9c2749-d0f2-4969-8842-53ff6b0de2e6) and sorcerers in the wider world however the entire body is affected by the transformation. This transformation is called “Ruakahu Uho” which means to become the core or center of Ruakahu becoming one with their god. In result of this transformation they are larger, stronger and able to regenerate themselves whilst transformed. When the Marike transform, they only have a few hours before their Uho slowly loses its power causing the warrior to slowly lose control of their body as the flesh bones and cells have now been changed into wood without the power of the Uho to move the wooden limbs the Marike lose the ability to return to their human form, losing the breath of life that was bestowed upon them by Ruakahu and becoming a tree once more.
