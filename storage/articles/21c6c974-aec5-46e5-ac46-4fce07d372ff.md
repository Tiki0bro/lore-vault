[h1]The Sacred Orders of The Conclave of the Pulse[/h1]
The Conclave of the Pulse is the dominant spiritual authority in the Artilian Kingdom, serving as the unifying body for human worship and the arbiter of divine law. It is served by two distinct, yet allied, sacred orders: the Theurges (spiritual leaders and spellcasters) and the Radiant Host (divine warriors). Both orders primarily serve the twin Greater Gods, Ethos and Khemos, reflecting their paramount importance in human society.

[h2]The Theurges of the Pulse (The Spirit Guides)[/h2]
The Theurges are the intellectual and spiritual core of The Conclave, responsible for divine interpretation, healing, blessings, and moral guidance. They are the primary petitioners to the gods, seeking to channel the Godpulse on behalf of the faithful.

[h2]The Two Sacred Orders[/h2]
The Theurges are internally divided into two distinct orders, each dedicated to a Greater God.

[h3]The Scepter of Ethos: [/h3]These Theurges are guided by the doctrine of Perfect Symmetry. They serve as legal advisors and inquisitors, focusing on law, justice, truth, and radiant magic. Their temples are grand and geometrically precise, mirroring their god's view of the Godpulse.

[h3]The Veil of Khemos: [/h3]These Theurges are guided by the doctrine of Cyclical Balance. They serve as healers, counselors, and purveyors of mysteries. Their magic focuses on protection, subtle manipulation, and restorative arts. Their temples, such as the Temple of Khemos Redcliff, are often serene and contemplative spaces.

[h3]Training and Purpose[/h3]
The training of a Theurge is focused on intensive spiritual and academic study of sacred texts, rituals, and the philosophies of their chosen deity. They learn to establish a clear connection to their god through prayer and meditation. Theurges of Lesser Gods (Zylos, Laria, etc.) exist but act as subsidiary clergy, answering to the higher-ranking Theurges of Ethos and Khemos. The Theurges of the Pulse are jointly led by an Arch-Theurge of Ethos and an Arch-Theurge of Khemos, who form the supreme spiritual authority in the kingdom.

[h2]The Radiant Host (The Divine Blades)[/h2]
The Radiant Host is the martial arm of The Conclave, comprised of elite warriors known as Pulsaris. They are the Vanguard of the Pulse, acting as the physical manifestation of divine justice and protection. They are exclusively devoted to Ethos or Khemos.

[h2]The Two Sacred Orders[/h2]
The Radiant Host is divided into two exclusive orders, each with a distinct combat philosophy.

[h3]The Sunsworn Knights:[/h3] Pulsaris dedicated to Ethos. Their training philosophy is "The Unbreakable Phalanx," emphasizing discipline, resilience, and methodical advance. They are clad in gleaming heavy armor, their weapons and bodies infused with the radiant energy of Ethos. They swear oaths of truth and justice, serving as righteous smiters of heretics and protectors of the innocent.

[h3]The Moonguard: [/h3]Pulsaris dedicated to Khemos. Their training philosophy is "The Silent Watch," focusing on agility, stealth, and strategic intervention. They wear lighter armor and specialize in subtle combat, dispelling curses, and protecting sacred sites. They operate covertly, relying on stealth and surprise to fulfill their sacred oaths.

[h3]Training and Purpose[/h3]
Pulsaris undergo a rigorous regimen of martial and spiritual training, learning to channel their deity's will directly into combat. Their oaths are unbreakable commitments to their gods, with their power directly tied to their unwavering faith. They are the frontline force that defends the kingdom from threats that defy the spiritual and legal authority of The Conclave, and their leadership is split between a Grand Marshal of Ethos and a Grand Marshal of Khemos.
