## 1. Feature Concept Overview
The **AI World Simulator & Causality Engine** allows creators to simulate era progressions (10, 50, 100 years), macro-historical events, faction conflict evolution, and dynamic town rumors based on actual vault articles. It incorporates Arthur Vance's Socratic "5-Whys" Causality Audit to spot narrative plot holes.

## 2. Persona Ideation Slate

- 🔍 **Arthur Vance (The Griller)**: "AI prose generation is often fluff. If this engine doesn't challenge worldbuilding logic—like asking why a teleportation-rich kingdom has merchant wagon trains—it's useless."
