# Dalari Lore Vault

> Complete world compendium and codex for **Dalari**, organized and extracted from World Anvil.

## 📖 World Overview

**Dalari** is a rich dark fantasy campaign setting where the gods once walked among mortals, leaving behind the sacred frequencies of the **Godpulse**, ancient relics, towering monoliths, and dangerous wild frontiers.

- **World Author**: Tiki0bro
- **Featured Campaign**: Tragedy at Redcliff
- **Total Articles**: 129
- **Total Extracted Artwork & Images**: 124

## 🚀 Lore Vault App Integration (2 Separate Vault Files)

Two dedicated, pristine import files are generated for complete modular separation:

1. **`dalari_codex_import.json` (World Codex — 103 Articles)**:
   - Pure setting compendium: Pantheon, Godpulse frequencies, magical schools, bestiary, cultures, settlements, and ancient relics.
   - 100% free of session logs, player briefing text, or campaign encounters.
2. **`silverhawks_campaign_import.json` (The Silverhawks Campaign — 19 Articles)**:
   - Campaign adventure ledger: 8 narrative session encounters (*The Gathering of the Silverhawks*, *Travelers in the night*, *Meeting Aaron Tabor*, *Ambushed in Merton*, *Crossing the Ingerrey River*, *The Guarded Bridge*, *The Shadow Stalker*, *Twinpine Garrison*), the Thistlewood Safehouse, the Silverhawks corp, intercepted clues (*Letter to Vadith*), and campaign target profiles (*Semarel*, *Aaron Tabor*, *Lord Ceowulf & sons*).

### Cleaned & Excluded Items (7 entries pruned):
- 4 World Anvil Tutorial Articles: *Welcome to World Anvil - READ ME FIRST*, *How to stage your world*, *Introduction to the Atlas*, *Introduction to the Encyclopedia*.
- 2 Scratchpads / Empty Prompts: *Trash Monster: name to be decided*, *Loot 1* (raw D&D Beyond URL dump).
- 1 Redundant Stub: *semarel* (empty stub; replaced by the complete *Semarel* profile).

### How to Import into Lore Vault:
1. Open Lore Vault in your browser (`npm run dev`).
2. Click **'Import World...'** in the top navigation bar.
3. Import **`dalari_codex_import.json`** using **'🧹 Clean Slate'** to establish your pure world vault.
4. Next, import **`silverhawks_campaign_import.json`** using **'✨ New Realm'** to add the campaign alongside it.
5. Use the Realm Switcher at the top of the app to switch between the world codex and the campaign anytime!
